## Superimposition

### Center of Attention

Increases the wearer's CRIT DMG by 48%. While the wearer is on the field, for every 1 Elation character in the team, increases the Skill Point upper limit by 1, up to a max increase of 3. For every 1 Skill Point the wearer consumes, enables the Elation DMG dealt by this unit to ignore #6[f1]% of enemy targets' DEF, stacking up to 4 times. If 4 or more Skill Points are consumed in the same turn, the wearer gains "Stream Promo," which increases all allies' Elation by 20%. Light Cone effects of the same type cannot stack.
