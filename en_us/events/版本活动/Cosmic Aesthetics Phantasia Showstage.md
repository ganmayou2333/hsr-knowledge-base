## Gameplay

During the event, Trailblazers can use Fashion Invites for draws in "Phantasia Showstage" to obtain Aeon ★ Aha's brand-new outfit "Star Lotto," as well as other rewards such as the Trailblaze Fashion - Back "Phantasmoon Star Rail," Namecard "Euphoria Time," and Chat Box "Cosmic Lotto."

Draw rules (eight prizes with no duplicates and removal on draw, the first draw being free, the rising per-draw cost and the rising outfit odds, the pity rule, and the Stellar Jade exchange rate) are published only in the official Chinese event page (miyoushe 79217286). The official English notice states that more information is in the dedicated Version 4.7 "Cosmic Aesthetics: Phantasia Showstage" post, which this batch could not retrieve; the step-by-step wording is therefore recorded as **Pending** rather than machine-translated.

Outfit notes:

- "Star Lotto" can only be used by Aeon ★ Aha.
- Obtaining the outfit also grants the matching outfit avatar.
- Only one copy of the outfit can be held; it cannot be obtained again.
- After equipping "Star Lotto," Aeon ★ Aha wears it during exploration and combat, and some abilities will have special effects.
- Whether or not the outfit is equipped, it does not affect the character's appearance in story cutscenes.
