## Gameplay

Leave "Fluffy Across the Blue" unfinished? Sorry, but the greatest and most responsible comic artist, Blurry_QR_Colt, could never do such a thing. In fact, she's even going to draw a gourmet story version! She has left behind an inheritance waiting to be claimed. To get your hands on it, you'll have to first finish this masterpiece. However, "drawing" comics isn't as simple as you think...

Step-by-step rules (running a dessert shop inside the comic world, filling customer orders to earn takings, upgrading shop equipment to unlock new ingredients and desserts, raising the manuscript progress of each chapter, and the split between limited-time and permanent rewards) are published only in the official Chinese event page (miyoushe 79468686); the shop's and the reward tiers' in-game names also exist only in Chinese. The official English notice carries no itemised rules, so they are recorded as **Pending** rather than machine-translated.

Notes on availability (from the same Chinese post):

- Once the limited-time event ends, its limited-time rewards can no longer be obtained, but the event itself remains playable in its scene and still grants the permanent rewards.
- After the limited-time period, the event is added to the permanent archive, where the gameplay can still be experienced.
