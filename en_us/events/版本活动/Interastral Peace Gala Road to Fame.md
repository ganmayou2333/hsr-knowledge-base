## Gameplay

Want to become the star of the show? Step onto the stage, take down your rivals, and win cheers and applause with your brilliance!

Step-by-step rules (five challenge stages unlocked day by day, each split into a warm-up phase and a final phase; trial characters usable; shields broken in the warm-up phase accumulate heat that activates buffs carried into the final phase; ratings and rating rewards; automatic character enhancement during the event) are published only in the official Chinese event page (miyoushe 79469418). The official English notice carries no itemised rules, so they are recorded as **Pending** rather than machine-translated.

Notes on availability (from the same Chinese post):

- During the event, limited-time rewards can be claimed in the "limited-time rewards" section after meeting the designated challenge goals inside a stage.
- During the event, reaching SS rating on all five stages unlocks a display-case collectible medal in the "commemorative medal" section (its in-game name exists only in Chinese → Pending).
