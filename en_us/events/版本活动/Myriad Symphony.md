## Gameplay

During the event, cumulatively consume Star Rail Special Passes or Fashion Invites to obtain Resonance and claim Affinitive Eidolons!

Values from the official Chinese event post (miyoushe 79217585; no official English text for these numbers):

- During the event, reaching 1600 and 3200 accumulated Resonance grants Affinitive Eidolon ×1 each.
- During the event, consuming 1 Star Rail Special Pass in any event warp grants 16 Resonance.
- During the event, consuming 1 Fashion Invite in the "Phantasia Showstage" event grants 10 Resonance.
- Rewards obtained through irregular means may be deducted by the Express Crew.

> Affinitive Eidolon is the special Eidolon added in Version 4.7: it grants characters brand-new abilities and combat performance, and unlocks character-themed illustrations on the Eidolon screen. Owning a character and their corresponding Affinitive Eidolon activates the Affinitive Eidolon, regardless of the character's original Eidolon Level (official English wording from the Version 4.7 Update Details, "8. Others · System").
