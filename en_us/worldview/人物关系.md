# Character Relationships

> Relationship chart of the major characters of Honkai: Star Rail (grouped by faction / planet)
> Status: v0.1 (2026-08-29 initial framework)
> Sources: in-game story, character stories, official settings

## Reading Guide

- Characters are grouped by faction / planet; each group lists core members and relationship chains.
- Relationship labels: **family** / **colleagues** / **friends** / **lovers** / **master & disciple** / **enemies** / **old friends** / **partners** / **unknown**.
- Avoid excessive spoilers; for detailed story, see [[worldview/剧情时间线|Story Timeline]] and each character's story.
- Character names are followed by their Path / element (e.g., "Kafka (Nihility · Lightning)").

## I. The Astral Express (core family)

### Core Members

| Character | Role | Path · Element | Relationship Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Himeko | Navigator / restorer | Erudition · Fire | De facto leader of the train; everyone's "big sister" |
| Welt Yang | Guide / combatant | Nihility · Imaginary | Former "First Herrscher" from another world; an experienced elder |
| March 7th | Crew member | Preservation · Ice | Amnesiac girl; the lively "little sister" |
| Dan Heng | Crew member | The Hunt · Wind | Mysterious young man burdened by his past; in truth the reincarnation of Imbibitor Lunae |
| The Trailblazer | Crew member | Switchable | Protagonist, Stellaron vessel, the player's avatar |
| Pom-Pom | Conductor | — | Mysterious little creature; the train's "steward" |

### Relationship Chains

- **Family**: the Express crew members are family to one another; Himeko is the leader, Welt is the elder, March 7th and Dan Heng are peers, and the Trailblazer is the newest member.
- **Himeko ↔ Welt**: colleagues / friends; they manage the train together, and Welt provides Himeko with experience from another world.
- **March 7th ↔ Dan Heng**: colleagues / friends; March 7th is curious about Dan Heng's past and often clings to him.
- **Dan Heng ↔ Trailblazer**: friends / comrades-in-arms; Dan Heng sees in the Trailblazer the possibility of change.
- **March 7th ↔ Trailblazer**: friends; March 7th was the first to warmly welcome the Trailblazer after they joined the train.
- **Pom-Pom ↔ everyone**: conductor-passenger relationship; Pom-Pom diligently manages the train's daily affairs.

### External Relationships

- **Himeko ↔ Herta**: friends / partners; Herta Space Station is an important resupply point for the train, and Himeko and Herta engage in academic exchange.
- **Welt ↔ Acheron**: acquaintances / unknown; Welt knows Acheron, and the two share past ties (a connection from another world).
- **Dan Heng ↔ Blade / Jingliu / Jing Yuan**: old grudges from the Luofu; see the "Xianzhou Luofu" section.
- **Trailblazer ↔ Kafka / Silver Wolf**: creators; Kafka and Silver Wolf implanted the Stellaron at Herta Space Station and awakened the Trailblazer.

### Core Members

| Blade | Swordsman | Destruction · Wind | Mara-Struck swordsman seeking death and vengeance |

### Relationship Chains

- **Colleagues**: the Stellaron Hunters act according to Elio's script, trusting one another yet each keeping secrets.
- **Elio ↔ everyone**: leader and subordinates; Elio's script is the highest principle of action.
- **Kafka ↔ Silver Wolf**: colleagues / partners; they often act together; Kafka looks after Silver Wolf, and Silver Wolf depends on Kafka.
- **Kafka ↔ Blade**: colleagues; Kafka is responsible for suppressing Blade's Mara-Struck; the two share a special bond.
- **Blade ↔ SAM**: colleagues; combat partners.
- **Silver Wolf ↔ Kafka**: a sister-like relationship; Silver Wolf calls Kafka "Ms. Kafka."

### External Relationships

- **Kafka ↔ Trailblazer**: creator / a mother-like figure; Kafka implanted the Stellaron and awakened the Trailblazer, holding special feelings toward them.
- **Silver Wolf ↔ Trailblazer**: creator / friend; Silver Wolf took part in awakening the Trailblazer and interacted with them in Penacony.
- **Blade ↔ Dan Heng**: enemies / old friends; Blade hunts down Dan Heng (the reincarnation of Imbibitor Lunae); the two have past grudges from the Luofu.
- **Blade ↔ Jingliu**: master & disciple; Jingliu is Blade's master, and Blade's Mara-Struck is connected to her.
- **Blade ↔ Jing Yuan**: old friends / enemies; Jing Yuan was Blade's old friend on the Luofu (the High-Cloud Quintet); they now stand opposed.
- **SAM ↔ Firefly**: the same person; Firefly's true identity is SAM of the Stellaron Hunters (armored form).
- **Acheron ↔ Stellaron Hunters**: former member / unknown; Acheron was once a member of the Stellaron Hunters, later leaving; she has old ties with Kafka.

### Relationship Chains

#### The High-Cloud Quintet (old friends / legends)
- **Members**: Jing Yuan, Blade (Yingxing), Jingliu, Dan Feng (Imbibitor Lunae), Tingyun (? / the fifth member pending confirmation)
- **Relationship**: once the most powerful warrior group of the Luofu; later torn apart by Dan Feng's sin (the Sedition of Imbibitor Lunae).
- **Current state**: Jing Yuan became General; Blade became a Stellaron Hunter (Mara-Struck); Jingliu wanders (Mara-Struck); Dan Heng was reborn as a member of the Express crew.

#### Core Relationships
- **Jing Yuan ↔ Blade**: old friends / enemies; Jing Yuan was Blade's old friend in the High-Cloud Quintet; they now stand opposed, and Jing Yuan has let Blade go multiple times.
- **Jing Yuan ↔ Jingliu**: old friends / master & disciple?; Jingliu was once Jing Yuan's master or senior; the two share a complicated past.
- **Jing Yuan ↔ Dan Heng (Dan Feng)**: old friends / lord and subject; Jing Yuan and Dan Feng were old friends in the High-Cloud Quintet; after Dan Heng's reincarnation, Jing Yuan chose to forgive and help him.
- **Blade ↔ Jingliu**: master & disciple; Jingliu is Blade's master; Blade's Mara-Struck is tied to Jingliu's fall, and Blade hunts her down.
- **Blade ↔ Dan Heng**: enemies / old friends; Blade hunts down Dan Heng (Dan Feng's reincarnation); the two share grudges from the Sedition of Imbibitor Lunae.
- **Jingliu ↔ Dan Feng**: old friends / enemies; Jingliu and Dan Feng were old friends in the High-Cloud Quintet; their relationship broke after the Sedition of Imbibitor Lunae.
- **Bailu ↔ Dan Feng**: same species / unknown; Bailu is a Vidyadhara dragon girl who may have a lineage connection to Dan Feng (Imbibitor Lunae).

#### Other Relationships
- **Jing Yuan ↔ Yanqing**: lord and subject / master & disciple; Yanqing is Jing Yuan's attendant and student; Jing Yuan teaches Yanqing swordsmanship.
- **Jing Yuan ↔ Fu Xuan**: lord and subject / succession; Fu Xuan is Jing Yuan's successor; the two work well together.
- **Fu Xuan ↔ Qingque**: superior and subordinate; Qingque is Fu Xuan's subordinate at the Divination Commission and often slacks off.
- **Xueyi ↔ Hanya**: sisters; Hanya is Xueyi's younger sister; both are Ten-Lords judges.
- **Huohuo ↔ Heliobus**: symbiosis; Huohuo seals a Heliobus (evil spirit) within her body; the two coexist.
- **Yukong ↔ Tingyun**: colleagues / friends; the two are colleagues on the Xianzhou; Yukong was grief-stricken after Tingyun's disappearance.

### External Relationships

- **Luocha ↔ Cocolia**: unknown / connected; Luocha is connected to the late Supreme Guardian of Belobog, Cocolia, and may be the one who revived her.
- **Jing Yuan ↔ Express crew**: allies; Jing Yuan cooperated with the crew to resolve the Luofu Stellaron crisis and chose to forgive Dan Heng (Dan Feng).
- **Dan Heng ↔ Express crew**: family; Dan Heng is a member of the crew and returned to the train after the Luofu incident.
- **Tingyun ↔ Phantylia**: replaced / enemies; Tingyun was replaced by Phantylia, the messenger of the Abundance Aeon; the real Tingyun's whereabouts are unknown.

## VIII. Key Cross-Faction Relationship Chains

### 1. The Luofu Grudge Chain (core story line)
```
Dan Feng (Imbibitor Lunae) — old friends — Jing Yuan (General)
    |                          |
 reincarnation/sin        old friends
    |                          |
Dan Heng (Express crew) — enemies — Blade (Stellaron Hunter) — master & disciple — Jingliu (wanderer)
                          |
                       Mara-Struck
```
- **Core event**: the Sedition of Imbibitor Lunae (Dan Feng's sin) tore apart the High-Cloud Quintet; Blade and Jingliu became Mara-Struck, and after his reincarnation Dan Heng was hunted by Blade.
- **Current state**: Jing Yuan chose to forgive Dan Heng; Blade is still hunting him but has been let go by Jing Yuan multiple times; Jingliu wanders, searching for a cure for Mara-Struck.

### 2. The Penacony Romance (core story line)
```
Robin (singer) — siblings — Sunday (observer)
    |
  idol/friend
    |
```

- **Current state**: Robin's fate is resolved in the v3.8 Final Mission; the relationship between Firefly and the Trailblazer continues to develop.

### 3. The Jarilo-VI Romance (confirmed lovers)

```
Bronya (Supreme Guardian) — lovers — Seele (Wildfire)
```
