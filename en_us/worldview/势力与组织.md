# Factions and Organizations

> Setting document for the major factions and organizations of Honkai: Star Rail
> Status: v0.1 (2026-08-29 initial framework)
> Sources: in-game story, official settings, HoYoLAB Wiki

## I. The Astral Express (星穹列车)

### Overview

- **Role**: an interstellar travel organization with "Trailblaze" as its mission, shuttling between worlds aboard the Astral Express to resolve Stellaron crises.
- **Founder**: Akivili (the Aeon of Trailblaze, deceased)
- **Current leader**: Himeko (navigator / de facto head of the train)
- **Base**: the Astral Express (a miraculous train that can traverse Star Rails)

### Core Members

| Member | Role | Path | Profile |
|---|---|---|---|
| Himeko | Navigator / restorer | Erudition | De facto leader of the Astral Express; restored the abandoned train; warm and outgoing. |
| Welt Yang | Guide / combatant | Nihility | Former "First Herrscher" from another world; rich in experience; the train's strategist. |
| March 7th | Crew member | Preservation | Mysterious girl frozen within Six-Phase Ice, having lost her past memories; lively. |
| Dan Heng | Crew member | The Hunt | Mysterious young man burdened by his past; in truth the reincarnation of the Xianzhou Dragon Lord, Imbibitor Lunae. |
| The Trailblazer (protagonist) | Crew member | Switchable | Special being implanted with a Stellaron, controlled by the player; can switch among multiple Paths. |
| Pom-Pom | Conductor | — | The Astral Express's conductor, a mysterious little creature in charge of the train's daily operations. |

### Goals and Mission

- Inherit the will of the Trailblaze Aeon Akivili and explore unknown Star Rails and worlds.
- Resolve the Stellaron crises of each world and help civilizations affected by Stellarons.
- Oppose the Antimatter Legion, the Stellaron Hunters, and other factions to maintain balance in the universe.

## II. The Antimatter Legion (反物质军团)

### Overview

- **Role**: an invading force whose goal is to destroy all civilizations, led by Nanook, the Aeon of Destruction.
- **Supreme ruler**: Nanook (the Aeon of Destruction)
- **Nature**: a universe-scale invading force

### Organizational Structure

#### 1. Nanook (the Aeon of Destruction)
- Supreme ruler of the Antimatter Legion, who regards civilizations as the "cancer" of the universe.
- Goal: trigger each world's self-destruction through Stellarons, ultimately returning the entire universe to nihility.

#### 2. The Stellaron Hunters (星核猎手)
- A mysterious organization hired by / cooperating with Nanook, responsible for deploying Stellarons on various worlds.
- Core members: Kafka, Silver Wolf, Blade, SAM, Elio (the scriptwriter).
- See the "Stellaron Hunters" section below.

#### 3. Antimatter soldiers
- **Voidrangers (虚卒)**: the Legion's basic infantry, divided into several types (Voidranger: Reaver, Voidranger: Desecrator, etc.).
- **Marauders (溃兵)**: fallen warriors eroded by the Legion's power.
- **Doomsday Beasts (末日兽)**: the Legion's high-level combatants, colossal monsters spawned by Stellaron power.
- **Others**: various creatures and machines reshaped by antimatter power.

### Methods of Action

- Deploy Stellarons on various worlds through the Stellaron Hunters, triggering internal collapse and disaster within civilizations.
- Directly dispatch the Antimatter Legion to invade weaker worlds with devastating strikes.
- Exploit internal conflicts in each world to accelerate civilizations' self-destruction.

## III. The Stellaron Hunters (星核猎手)

### Overview

- **Role**: a mysterious organization active across worlds, hired by Nanook (or other factions) to deploy Stellarons and steer fate.
- **Leader**: Elio (the scriptwriter, able to foresee the future)
- **Nature**: mercenaries / fateshapers

### Core Members

| Member | Title / Role | Path | Profile |
|---|---|---|---|
| Elio | Scriptwriter | — | The behind-the-scenes leader of the Stellaron Hunters; can foresee the future and devise the organization's action "scripts." |
| Kafka | Core member | Nihility | Mysterious woman with mental-control abilities; composed and elegant; the Stellaron Hunters' representative figure. |
| Silver Wolf | Hacker / augmented human | Nihility | Genius hacker who treats reality like a game she can modify; easygoing; skilled at planting weaknesses and rewriting reality. |
| Blade | Swordsman | Destruction | Swordsman cursed with "Mara-Struck," with past grudges against Dan Heng; seeks death and vengeance. |
| SAM | Warrior | — | Warrior clad in heavy armor; the Stellaron Hunters' main combatant. |

### Goals and Actions

- Deploy Stellarons on various worlds according to Elio's "script" to steer the course of fate.
- Both friends and foes of the Astral Express; sometimes cooperate over shared interests.
- Each member has their own aims and past; the organization's internal relationships are complex.

## IV. The Interastral Peace Corporation (IPC, 星际和平公司)

### Overview

- **Role**: the largest commercial / financial monopoly in the universe, controlling the economies of countless worlds in the name of "peace."
- **Leaders**: the Ten Stonehearts (the company's highest decision-making body)
- **Symbols**: Credits (the universe's common currency), contracts, commercial order
- **Associated Aeon**: Qlipoth of Preservation (the company maintains commercial order in the name of "Preservation")

### Organizational Structure

#### 1. The Ten Stonehearts
- The company's highest decision-making body, composed of its ten most powerful decision-makers.
- Members include: Diamond (the company's current head), Jade (head of the Strategic Investment Department), Aventurine (head of the Strategic Partnership Department), and others.

#### 2. Departments
- **Strategic Investment Department**: headed by Jade, handling the company's major investments and acquisitions.
- **Strategic Partnership Department**: headed by Aventurine, handling cooperation and negotiations with various factions.
- **Other departments**: including Credit issuance, contract management, and armed enforcement.

#### 3. Armed forces
- **Company mercenaries**: armed forces hired by the company to protect its commercial interests.
- **Topaz & Numby**: the company's special debt collectors, responsible for recovering debts from various worlds.

### Goals and Actions

- Control the economic lifelines of various worlds through Credits, debts, contracts, and other means.
- Maintain the company's commercial monopoly in the name of "peace" and "order."
- Both cooperate and conflict with factions such as the Astral Express and the Xianzhou Alliance.

## V. The Xianzhou Alliance (仙舟联盟)

### Overview

- **Role**: an interstellar alliance of six Xianzhou ships, aiming to hunt down the Abundance Aeon Yaoshi and cure Mara-Struck.
- **Origin**: in ancient times, seekers of immortality rode the Xianzhou ships in search of the elixir of life; after receiving the Abundance blessing, they fell into the curse of Mara-Struck instead.
- **Nature**: a military / civilization alliance

### The Six Xianzhou Ships

| Xianzhou ship | Name | Traits | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Xianzhou Luofu | Luofu | Core of the alliance; homeland of the Dragon Lord and the Vidyadhara | Active |
| Xianzhou Yaoqing | Yaoqing | Renowned for military might | Active |
| Xianzhou Zhuming | Zhuming | Renowned for industry / forging | Active |
| Xianzhou Xuling | Xuling | Mysterious; little information | Active |
| Xianzhou Fanghu | Fanghu | Destroyed by Abundance monstrosities | Fallen |
| Xianzhou Yuque | Yuque | Missing / fallen | Missing |

### Core Factions (taking the Xianzhou Luofu as an example)

#### 1. The Cloud Knights
- The Xianzhou's official military force, responsible for defending the ships and hunting Abundance monstrosities.
- Notable members: Jing Yuan (General of the Luofu / the Divine Foresight), Yanqing (Cloud Knight lieutenant), Sushang (Cloud Knight), etc.

#### 2. The Vidyadhara
- A native species of the Xianzhou Luofu, bearing draconic traits and able to be reborn through reincarnation.
- Leader: the Dragon Lord, Imbibitor Lunae (passed down through generations; the current one is the reincarnation of Dan Heng / Dan Feng).
- Home: Scalegorge Waterscape.

#### 3. The Foxians
- One of the Xianzhou's species, bearing fox traits and excelling at scouting and guerrilla warfare.
- Notable members: Tingyun (emissary), Yukong (Helmsmaster of the Sky-Faring Commission), etc.

#### 4. The Ten-Lords Commission
- The Xianzhou's judiciary / underworld management body, responsible for judging departed souls and maintaining order.
- Notable members: Xueyi (Ten-Lords judge), Hanya (Ten-Lords judge), etc.

#### 5. The Disciples of Sanctus Medicus
- An underground organization on the Xianzhou that worships the Abundance Aeon Yaoshi and pursues immortality.
- Hostile to the Xianzhou Alliance; one of the forces behind the Luofu Stellaron crisis.

### Goals and Actions

- Hunt down the Abundance Aeon Yaoshi and eliminate the root of Mara-Struck.
- Defend the Xianzhou Alliance and fight the Abundance monstrosities and the Antimatter Legion.
- Cooperate with the Astral Express during the Luofu incident to resolve the Stellaron crisis together.

## VI. The Intelligentsia Guild (博识学会)

### Overview

- **Role**: an academic organization founded by the Erudition Aeon Nous, gathering the smartest geniuses in the universe.
- **Founder**: Nous (the Aeon of Erudition)
- **Nature**: an academic / research organization

### Core Members

| Member | Title | Profile |
|---|---|---|
| Herta | Genius Society #83 | Owner of the space station; genius scientist who operates through puppet bodies; tsundere by nature. |
| Ruan Mei | Genius Society #89 | Scientist from the Xianzhou, versed in life engineering; gentle and mysterious. |
| Screwllum | Genius Society #? | Mechanical genius who owns his own planet. |
| Other members | — | About 100+ geniuses in total, each with their own field of expertise. |

### Base

- **Herta Space Station**: an important base of the Intelligentsia Guild, presided over by Herta, housing a large collection of cosmic wonders and research results.
- The starting point of the Astral Express story, where the Trailblazer was awakened.

### Goals and Actions

- Pursue the ultimate truth of the universe and conduct cutting-edge research.
- Members research independently, occasionally cooperating over shared interests.
- Friendly toward the Astral Express; Herta Space Station is an important resupply point for the train.

## VII. Other Major Factions

### 1. The Memokeepers (忆者; memory-related organization)
- A mysterious organization tied to the Remembrance Path, dedicated to collecting / protecting / manipulating memories.
- Connected to factions such as Penacony and the Golden-Blooded (黄金裔).
- Detailed settings await further official revelation.

### 2. The Masked Fools (假面愚者)
- An organization founded by the Elation Aeon Aha, dedicated to "finding amusement in the universe."
- Members act absurdly and unpredictably; a destabilizing factor in the universe.
- Representative figure: Sparkle (member of the Masked Fools).

### 3. The Golden-Blooded (黄金裔; related organization)
- A faction connected to Penacony and the Remembrance Path.
- Detailed settings await further official revelation.

### 4. Local factions of each world
- **Jarilo-VI**: Belobog (the Supreme Guardian / Silvermane Guards / Wildfire).
- **Penacony**: the Family (the Eight Lawkeepers), the Hound Family (猎犬家族), the Clockmaker, etc.
- **Amphoreus**: the city-states and tribes.
