## I. Core Concepts

### Stellaron (星核)
- Mysterious objects known as the "cancer of all worlds"; after descending upon a world, they fuse with the world's core and trigger disasters (e.g., Jarilo-VI's eternal winter, the Xianzhou Luofu's Abundance monstrosities).
- The Trailblazer has a Stellaron implanted inside, making them a special "Stellaron vessel."
- The Stellaron Hunters are hired to deploy Stellarons on various worlds, steering the course of fate.

### Aeon (星神)
- The most powerful beings in the universe; each governs a **Path** (philosophy / law).
- Aeons can die (e.g., Tayzzyronth of Propagation, Ena of Order), sleep (e.g., Oroboros of Nihility), or go missing (e.g., Idrila of Harmony).
- See [[zh_cn/worldview/星神与命途|Aeons and Paths]].

### Path (命途)
- The philosophy / law governed by an Aeon; also the source of characters' power.
- Known Paths: Preservation, Destruction, Erudition, Harmony, Nihility, Abundance, The Hunt, Remembrance, Elation, Propagation, Permanence, Order, Enigmata, Equilibrium, etc.
- A character's Path determines their combat role (e.g., Preservation = tank, The Hunt = single-target DPS).
- See [[zh_cn/worldview/星神与命途|Aeons and Paths]].

### Pathstrider (命途行者)
- A being who practices the ideals of a Path can gain its power.
- A character's "Path" is the road they walk.

### Star Rail (星轨)
- Interstellar routes connecting various worlds; the Astral Express travels between worlds along Star Rails.
- Blazing and maintaining Star Rails is an important part of the "Trailblaze" mission.

### Trailblaze (开拓)
- The mission of the Astral Express: to inherit the will of the Trailblaze Aeon Akivili, explore unknown Star Rails and worlds, and resolve Stellaron crises.
- The protagonist is called the "Trailblazer."

### Antimatter Legion (反物质军团) 【faction】
- An invading force led by Nanook, the Aeon of Destruction, whose goal is to "destroy all civilizations."
- See [[zh_cn/worldview/势力与组织|Factions and Organizations]].

### Stellaron Hunters (星核猎手) 【faction】
- A mysterious organization active across worlds, hired to deploy Stellarons and steer fate.
- Core members: Kafka, Silver Wolf, Blade, SAM, Elio (the scriptwriter).
- See [[zh_cn/worldview/势力与组织|Factions and Organizations]].

## II. Characters and Species

### Trailblazer (开拓者)
- The protagonist controlled by the player, awakened at Herta Space Station after Kafka and Silver Wolf implanted a Stellaron.
- Can switch among multiple Paths: Preservation, Destruction, Harmony, Nihility, Elation, Remembrance, etc.
- Comes in two forms: male (Caelus, 穹) and female (Stelle, 星).

### Long-lived / short-lived species (长生种 / 短生种)
- **Long-lived species**: species with extremely long lifespans, such as the Xianzhou natives (blessed by Abundance) and the Vidyadhara.
- **Short-lived species**: species with normal lifespans, such as ordinary humans.
- One of the Xianzhou Alliance's core contradictions: the longevity of the long-lived species brought the curse of Mara-Struck.

### Mara-Struck (魔阴身)
- A mental / physical mutation afflicting the Xianzhou natives (a long-lived species) due to their excessive lifespan; it causes people to lose their sanity and turn into monsters.
- Blade is Mara-Struck and relies on special means to maintain his sanity.
- One of the Xianzhou Alliance's core goals is to find a cure for Mara-Struck.

### Vidyadhara (持明族)
- One of the native species of the Xianzhou Luofu, bearing draconic traits and able to be reborn through reincarnation.
- Led by the Dragon Lord, Imbibitor Lunae, passed down through generations.
- Home: Scalegorge Waterscape.

### The Dragon Lord / Imbibitor Lunae (龙尊 / 饮月君)
- The supreme leader of the Vidyadhara, possessing great power; successive Dragon Lords are called "Imbibitor Lunae."
- Dan Heng's true identity is the reincarnation of the previous Imbibitor Lunae, Dan Feng.
- Dan Heng can awaken into the "Dan Heng • Imbibitor Lunae" form through special power.

### Golden-Blooded (黄金裔) 【faction】
- A special species / faction of Amphoreus, possessing the legacy of the gods; core setting of v3.0.
- Representative characters: Aglaea, Tribbie, Cyrene, Chang Geng (长庚), etc.

### Stellaron vessel (星核容器)
- A special being with a Stellaron implanted inside, such as the Trailblazer.
- Stellaron vessels can use the Stellaron's power, but also face the risk of being eroded by it.

## III. Combat Mechanics 【combat】

### Damage Type (属性)
- Attack attributes; 7 types in total: Physical, Fire, Ice, Lightning, Wind, Quantum, Imaginary.
- A character's type determines the attribute of their attacks, affecting weakness hits and resistance resolution.

### Weakness (弱点)
- An enemy's vulnerability to a specific attribute; attacks of the matching attribute reduce Toughness.
- Attacks of non-weakness attributes do not reduce Toughness (with some skill / Light Cone exceptions).

### Toughness (韧性)
- The enemy's Toughness bar, reduced by attacks of weakness attributes.
- When Toughness reaches zero, a **Weakness Break** triggers, and the enemy enters a weakened state.

### Weakness Break (击破)
- Triggers when Toughness reaches zero, dealing Break damage with an attribute effect.
- Break effects by attribute: Fire → Burn, Ice → Freeze, Lightning → Shock, Wind → Wind Shear, Physical → Bleed, Quantum → Entanglement (action delay), Imaginary → Imprisonment (SPD reduction).
- Break damage is based on the breaker's level and the target's Toughness cap, and is not directly related to ATK (special case: Super Break).

### Skill Point (战技点)
- A battle resource; Basic ATK restores 1 point, Skill consumes 1 point.
- Skill Points are shared by the team, with a cap of usually 5 points.

### Energy (能量)
- A character's Ultimate resource; the Ultimate can be unleashed once the cap is reached.
- Recovery: Basic ATK 20, Skill 30, being hit 10 (AoE 5), kill 10, Ultimate 5.
- See [[zh_cn/rules/通用战斗逻辑|General Combat Logic · Section 8]].

### Ultimate (终结技)
- A character's ultimate move, unleashed by consuming Energy; can be activated at any moment (turn interruption).
- Some Ultimates provide enhanced states, extra turns, or special mechanics.

### Follow-up Attack (追加攻击)
- Additional attacks automatically triggered when specific conditions are met; they do not consume Skill Points and do not occupy a normal action turn.
- Representative characters: Herta (enemy HP < 50%), Himeko (after 3 cumulative Weakness Breaks), Clara (counter on being hit), Jing Yuan (Lightning-Lord stack), etc.
- See [[zh_cn/rules/异常特例库|Exception Database · Entry 5]].

### Damage over Time (DoT, 持续伤害)
- Damage settled at the start of the turn, lasting for several turns.
- Types: Burn (Fire), Shock (Lightning), Bleed (Physical), Wind Shear (Wind), Quantum Entanglement, Imaginary Imprisonment, etc.
- Usually not affected by crits (special cases are registered in the Exception Database).
- See [[zh_cn/rules/状态优先级|Status Priority · Section 3]].

### Buff / Debuff (增益 / 减益)
- **Buff**: a positive status that enhances allied attributes or abilities.
- **Debuff**: a negative status that weakens the target or makes it take damage.
- Effects with the same name stack by type, keeping the highest value from the same source; different sources can coexist.

### Control Status (控制状态)
- Negative statuses that affect the target's actions, including: Freeze, Stun, Entanglement, Imprisonment, Taunt, heal block, Slow, action delay (pushback), etc.
- **Restraint (拘束)** has the highest priority and is judged independently.
- See [[zh_cn/rules/状态优先级|Status Priority · Section 2]].

### Effect Hit Rate / Effect RES (效果命中 / 效果抵抗)
- The hit-judgment attribute for applying debuffs.
- Formula: `Actual Proc Chance = Base Chance × (1 + Effect Hit Rate%) × (1 - Effect RES%) × (1 - Special Effect RES%)`.
- Debuffs whose skill description does not state a base chance always hit.
- See [[zh_cn/rules/通用战斗逻辑|General Combat Logic · Section 7]].

### Speed / Action Value (速度 / 行动值) 【combat】
- Speed determines action order; the higher the Speed, the more frequently actions occur.
- Formula: `Action Value = 10000 / Speed`.
- Action advance / action delay directly change the position on the action bar.
- See [[zh_cn/rules/通用战斗逻辑|General Combat Logic · Section 6]].

### Crit Rate / Crit DMG (暴击率 / 暴击伤害)
- Crit Rate determines whether this hit is a crit, capped at 100%.
- Crit DMG: on a crit, damage = base damage × (1 + Crit DMG%).

### Damage Multipliers (伤害乘区)
- The independent multiplicative areas of damage calculation, including: base damage, DMG boost, damage reduction, DEF reduction, RES, vulnerability, crit, etc.
- The areas multiply with one another; bonuses of the same kind add up within the same area.

### Eidolon (星魂)
- A character's breakthrough level; 6 levels total (E0–E6), enhancing abilities or unlocking new mechanics.
- Unlocked by obtaining duplicate copies of the same character.

### Traces (行迹)
- A character's skill tree, including Basic ATK, Skill, Ultimate, Talent, and bonus abilities (attribute boosts).
- Upgraded with materials to enhance skill effects or unlock passive abilities.

### Technique (秘技)
- Special skills used in the overworld (outside combat), consuming Technique Points.
- Effects include: buffs when entering battle, immediately attacking enemies, restoring HP, scene interaction, etc.

## IV. Factions and Organizations 【faction】

### Astral Express (星穹列车) 【faction】
- An interstellar travel organization with "Trailblaze" as its mission, shuttling between worlds aboard the Astral Express.
- Core members: Himeko, Welt, March 7th, Dan Heng, the Trailblazer, Pom-Pom.
- See [[zh_cn/worldview/势力与组织|Factions and Organizations]].

### IPC (Interastral Peace Corporation, 星际和平公司) 【faction】
- The largest commercial / financial monopoly in the universe, controlling the economies of countless worlds in the name of "peace."
- Highest decision-making body: the Ten Stonehearts (Diamond, Jade, Aventurine, etc.).
- See [[zh_cn/worldview/势力与组织|Factions and Organizations]].

### Xianzhou Alliance (仙舟联盟) 【faction】
- An interstellar alliance of six Xianzhou ships, aiming to hunt down the Abundance Aeon Yaoshi and cure Mara-Struck.
- Core ships: Luofu (core of the alliance), Yaoqing, Zhuming, Xuling, Fanghu (fallen), Yuque (missing).
- See [[zh_cn/worldview/势力与组织|Factions and Organizations]].

### Cloud Knights (云骑军) 【faction】
- The Xianzhou's official military force, responsible for defending the ships and hunting Abundance monstrosities.
- Generals of the Luofu: Jing Yuan (former), Fu Xuan (current).

### Intelligentsia Guild (博识学会) 【faction】
- An academic organization founded by the Erudition Aeon Nous, gathering the smartest geniuses in the universe.
- Core members: Herta (#83), Ruan Mei (#89), Screwllum, etc.
- Base: Herta Space Station.

### Genius Society (天才俱乐部)
- The core member organization of the Intelligentsia Guild, numbered (#1–#?), with each member a genius in their own field.

### Masked Fools (假面愚者) 【faction】
- An organization founded by the Elation Aeon Aha, dedicated to "finding amusement in the universe."
- Members act absurdly and unpredictably.
- Representative character: Sparkle.

### Memokeepers (忆者) 【faction】
- A mysterious organization tied to the Remembrance Path, dedicated to collecting / protecting / manipulating memories.
- Connected to factions such as Penacony and the Golden-Blooded.

### The Family (家族) 【faction】
- The ruling organization of Penacony, composed of the Eight Lawkeepers, controlling the order of the dreamscape planet.
- Core members: Sunday (the observer), Robin (lead singer of the Charmony Festival), etc.

### Disciples of Sanctus Medicus (药王秘传) 【faction】
- An underground organization on the Xianzhou that worships the Abundance Aeon Yaoshi and pursues immortality.
- Hostile to the Xianzhou Alliance; one of the forces behind the Luofu Stellaron crisis.

### Ten-Lords Commission (十王司) 【faction】
- The Xianzhou's judiciary / underworld management body, responsible for judging departed souls and maintaining order.
- Representative characters: Xueyi, Hanya (judges).

## V. Locations and Worlds 【location】

### Astral Express (星穹列车) 【location】
- A legacy left by the Trailblaze Aeon Akivili; a miraculous train capable of traversing Star Rails.
- The Express crew's base and mobile home, including areas such as the party car.

### Herta Space Station (黑塔空间站) 【location】
- An important research base of the Intelligentsia Guild, presided over by Herta.
- The starting point of the game, where the Trailblazer was awakened.
- See [[zh_cn/worldview/星球与地点|Planets and Locations · Section 1]].

### Jarilo-VI (雅利洛-VI) 【location】
- An ice planet shrouded in an eternal winter; humanity lives in the last city, Belobog.
- The main stage of the v1.0~1.1 main story.
- See [[zh_cn/worldview/星球与地点|Planets and Locations · Section 2]].

### Belobog (贝洛伯格) 【location】
- Jarilo-VI's last human city, divided into the upper city (administrative / commercial districts) and the lower city (underground mines).
- Ruled by: the Supreme Guardian (Cocolia → Bronya).

### Xianzhou Luofu (仙舟罗浮) 【location】
- One of the six Xianzhou ships; the core of the Xianzhou Alliance, blending Eastern xianxia and sci-fi elements.
- The main stage of the v1.2~1.3 main story.
- See [[zh_cn/worldview/星球与地点|Planets and Locations · Section 3]].

### Penacony (匹诺康尼) 【location】
- Once an IPC prison planet, now a "paradise of pleasure" dreamscape planet; prosperity on the surface, darkness hidden beneath.
- The main stage of the v2.0~2.3 main story.
- See [[zh_cn/worldview/星球与地点|Planets and Locations · Section 4]].

### Amphoreus (翁法罗斯) 【location】
- A world abandoned by the gods; new region in v3.0, mythological in style, homeland of the Golden-Blooded.
- The main stage of the v3.0~3.7 main story.
- See [[zh_cn/worldview/星球与地点|Planets and Locations · Section 5]].

### Duality Paradise (二相乐园) 【location】
- A new region in v4.0, tied to the Elation Path; the stage of the Masked Fools, possessing dual aspects (surface and reverse).
- The main stage of the v4.0~ main story (ongoing). Official English name pending.

### Simulated Universe (模拟宇宙)
- A virtual space developed by Herta for simulating battles with Aeons and Paths; players can obtain Blessings and Curios for roguelike gameplay.
- Versions: the classic Simulated Universe, Swarm Disaster, Gold and Gears, Divergent Universe, Unknowable Domain, etc.

## VI. Items and Resources

### Light Cone (光锥)
- One of a character's equipment slots, providing base stats (HP / ATK / DEF) and special effects.
- Classified by Path (Preservation Light Cones, Destruction Light Cones, etc.); characters of the same Path can activate special effects when equipping them.
- Rarity: 3-star / 4-star / 5-star.

### Relic (遗器)
- One of a character's equipment slots, providing stat bonuses and set effects.
- Slots: Head (HP), Hands (ATK), Body (DEF / Crit / Effect Hit Rate), Feet (SPD / HP / ATK), Planar Sphere (elemental DMG / DEF), Link Rope (Energy Regen / ATK / HP).
- Set effects: activated with 2-piece / 4-piece sets.

### Stellar Jade (星琼)
- The game's premium currency, used for Warps (gacha), purchasing Trailblaze Power, etc.
- Ways to obtain: missions, achievements, events, top-ups (Nameless Honor).

### Credit (信用点)
- The game's basic currency, used for character upgrades, Light Cone / Relic enhancement, shop purchases, etc.
- Ways to obtain: combat, missions, shops, events.

### Star Rail Pass / Star Rail Special Pass (星轨通票 / 星轨专票)
- Warp (gacha) items:
  - Star Rail Pass: used for the permanent Warp (Stellar Warp).
  - Star Rail Special Pass: used for character event Warps and Light Cone event Warps.

### Fuel (燃料) / Trailblaze Power (开拓力)
- The stamina consumed when challenging instances (Calyx, Stagnant Shadow, Echo of War, etc.).
- Natural recovery: 1 point every 5 minutes, cap 240 points.
- Fuel immediately restores 60 points of Trailblaze Power.

### Eidolon (星魂)
- See "III. Combat Mechanics."

### Trace Materials (行迹材料)
- Materials consumed to upgrade character Traces, categorized by attribute / world level.
- Ways to obtain: Stagnant Shadow, Calyx (Crimson), events, shops.

## VII. Gameplay and Systems

### Warp (跃迁)
- The game's gacha system, consuming Star Rail Passes / Special Passes to obtain characters and Light Cones.
- Types: character event Warp (rate-up banner), Light Cone event Warp (weapon banner), Stellar Warp (permanent banner), beginner Warp.
- Pity mechanics: 90 pulls for the soft pity (50% chance of the rate-up), 180 pulls for the hard pity (guaranteed rate-up).

### Forgotten Hall (忘却之庭)
- A high-difficulty challenge mode, divided into Memory of Chaos and Pure Fiction.
- Memory of Chaos: 12 floors, 3 stars per floor, testing team damage output and survival.
- Pure Fiction: rotates periodically, testing specific team compositions.

### Apocalyptic Shadow (异相仲裁)
- A high-difficulty mode added in v3.6, testing the team's response to specific mechanics.

### Aetherium Wars (货币战争)
- An auto-chess / card battle mode added in v3.7, using character cards to battle.
- See the `货币战争/` knowledge base.

### Pathstrider Ensemble (命路群像)
- A story system added in v2.1, allowing players to switch between different characters' perspectives in specific missions to advance the story.

### Triple Path System (三命途系统)
- Added in v2.0; the protagonist (Trailblazer) can switch among the Preservation / Destruction / Harmony Paths (later expanded to Nihility, Elation, Remembrance, etc.).

### Companion Mission (同行任务)
- Personal story missions for characters, providing deeper insight into their backgrounds and stories.
- Completion usually rewards a character Eidolon or materials.

### Trailblaze Mission (开拓任务)
- The game's main story missions, advancing the core plot.
- Grouped by planet / story arc.

### Trailblaze Continuance (开拓续闻)
- Additional story content after the main story, usually focusing on the follow-up developments of specific characters or planets.

### Final Mission (终末任务)
- The concluding missions of a specific planet's arc, bringing the entire arc to a close.

### Adventure Mission (冒险任务)
- The game's side missions, enriching the worldview and character stories.

### Daily Training (每日实训)
- The daily quest system; completion rewards Stellar Jade, Credits, materials, etc.

## VIII. Miscellaneous

### Pom-Pom (帕姆)
- The conductor of the Astral Express, a mysterious little creature in charge of the train's daily operations.
- Earnest in nature, calling himself "the conductor."

### Nameless (无名客)
- The title for people who follow the Trailblaze Aeon Akivili and travel aboard the Astral Express.
- The Trailblazer is called a "Nameless."

### The Stellaron Hunters' Script (星核猎手的剧本)
- The future foreseen by Elio (the scriptwriter); the Stellaron Hunters act according to the script, steering the course of fate.
- The script is not entirely fixed; there are variables and choices.

### Genesis Vortex (创世涡心)
- A core setting of Amphoreus, closely tied to the creation myth and the character Phainon (白厄).

### The Flame-Chase (逐火之旅)
- The core narrative of Amphoreus; the journey of the Flame-Chase in which the Flame Reaver leads mortals to challenge the legacy of the gods.

### Titan (泰坦)
- Ancient beings of Amphoreus, tied to the legacy of the gods and the creation myth.
