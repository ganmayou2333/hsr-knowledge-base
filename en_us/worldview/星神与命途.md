## I. Overview of the Aeons

### What Are Aeons

- **Aeons** are the most powerful beings in the universe; each governs a **Path** (a philosophy / law) and influences the entire universe through the power of that Path.
- An Aeon's birth is tied to "ascension": when a being's practice of an ideal reaches its extreme, that being may ascend to become an Aeon, governing the corresponding Path.
- Aeons are not immortal: Aeons can die (e.g., the Swarm Emperor of Propagation was hunted down, and Ena of Order was absorbed by the Harmony Aeon), fall asleep (e.g., Oroboros of Nihility), or go missing (e.g., the Harmony Aeon Idrila).

### The Behavior of Aeons

- Aeons usually do not directly intervene in mortal affairs; instead, they influence the universe indirectly through Pathstriders, messengers, and factions.
- Some Aeons have clear goals (e.g., Nanook wants to destroy all civilizations, while Qlipoth wants to protect civilization), while others are inscrutable (e.g., Aha, who delights in mischief).
- Aeons conflict and cooperate with one another: e.g., the opposition between Nanook of Destruction and Qlipoth of Preservation, and the Harmony Aeon absorbing Ena of Order.

## II. Paths in Detail

### 1. Preservation (存护)

- **Aeon**: Qlipoth (the Amber Lord)
- **Core ideal**: protection, defense, bulwarks, eternity
- **Aeon status**: alive
- **Representative characters**: Trailblazer (Preservation), Gepard, March 7th, Fu Xuan, Fire Trailblazer, Pom-Pom (Preservation)
- **Combat role**: tank / shields / damage reduction
- **Lore notes**: Qlipoth is one of the oldest Aeons, symbolized by "amber," devoted to protecting civilization from the Antimatter Legion's destruction. Preservation Pathstriders excel at creating shields and reducing damage.

### 2. Destruction (毁灭)

- **Aeon**: Nanook (Aeon of Destruction)
- **Core ideal**: destruction, ruin, finality, nothingness
- **Aeon status**: alive
- **Representative characters**: Trailblazer (Destruction), Arlan, Hook, Seele (Destruction), Blade (Destruction)
- **Combat role**: high-risk, high-reward DPS / offense-defense hybrid
- **Lore notes**: Nanook regards civilizations as the "cancer" of the universe and leads the Antimatter Legion to destroy all civilizations. Destruction Pathstriders typically trade self-harm or high risk for powerful damage output.

### 3. Erudition (智识)

- **Aeon**: Nous (the Genius Aeon / Aeon of Erudition)
- **Core ideal**: knowledge, wisdom, truth, exploration
- **Aeon status**: alive
- **Representative characters**: Himeko, Herta, Qingque, Jing Yuan, The Herta
- **Combat role**: AoE damage / multi-target damage
- **Lore notes**: Nous seeks the ultimate truth of the universe and founded the "Intelligentsia Guild." Erudition Pathstriders excel at dealing AoE damage, usually with bounce, blast, and similar mechanics.

### 4. Harmony (同谐)

- **Aeon**: Idrila (Aeon of Beauty / Harmony Aeon) — official lore names the Harmony Aeon as Xipe; Idrila is the Aeon of Beauty
- **Core ideal**: harmony, resonance, connection, beauty
- **Aeon status**: missing (whereabouts unknown)
- **Representative characters**: Trailblazer (Harmony), Bronya, Tingyun, Robin, Argenti, Firefly (Harmony)
- **Combat role**: support / buffs / action advance
- **Lore notes**: Idrila is also called the "Aeon of Beauty"; she once absorbed Ena of Order, unifying order and harmony. After Idrila vanished, the Harmony Path is sustained by the laws she left behind. Harmony Pathstriders excel at providing allies with buffs, speed boosts, and action advance.

### 5. Nihility (虚无)

- **Aeon**: Oroboros (Aeon of Nihility / the "Silent Aeon")
- **Core ideal**: nihility, negation, extinction, silence
- **Aeon status**: sleeping
- **Representative characters**: Welt Yang, Sampo, Pela, Silver Wolf, Kafka, Black Swan, Jade
- **Combat role**: debuffs / crowd control / damage over time
- **Lore notes**: Oroboros is one of the most mysterious Aeons, symbolizing that "all things will eventually return to nihility." Nihility Pathstriders excel at applying debuffs, crowd control, and damage over time to weaken enemies.

### 6. Abundance (丰饶)

- **Aeon**: Yaoshi (Aeon of Abundance)
- **Core ideal**: life, healing, endlessness, reproduction
- **Aeon status**: alive
- **Representative characters**: Natasha, Bailu, Luocha, Huohuo, Lynx, Aglaea
- **Combat role**: healing / recovery / sustain
- **Lore notes**: Yaoshi aims to "grant endless life," yet the blessing also brought disaster (e.g., the Mara-Struck affliction of the Xianzhou natives and the Abundance monstrosities). Abundance Pathstriders excel at healing, recovery, and sustain, but Yaoshi's power is also seen as a double-edged sword.

### 7. The Hunt (巡猎)

- **Aeon**: Lan (Aeon of the Hunt / the Reignbow Arbiter)
- **Core ideal**: vengeance, pursuit, swiftness, judgment
- **Aeon status**: alive
- **Representative characters**: Dan Heng, Seele (The Hunt), Yanqing, Sushang, Topaz, Acheron
- **Combat role**: single-target DPS / high burst / follow-up attacks
- **Lore notes**: Lan was once an ordinary archer who ascended to Aeonhood by exacting vengeance on the Abundance Aeon, and is worshiped by the Xianzhou under the title of the "Reignbow Arbiter." The Hunt Pathstriders excel at dealing high single-target burst damage, usually with follow-up attacks and enhanced basic attacks.

### 8. Remembrance (记忆)

- **Aeon**: Focalors (?) / the Remembrance Aeon (exact name pending confirmation)
- **Core ideal**: memory, recollection, history, legacy
- **Aeon status**: pending confirmation
- **Representative characters**: Trailblazer (Remembrance), The Herta (Remembrance), Busitu (不死途)
- **Combat role**: special mechanics / memory-related
- **Lore notes**: Remembrance is a newer Path, connected to factions such as the "Memokeepers" and the "Golden-Blooded" (黄金裔). Remembrance Pathstriders' abilities involve the manipulation, extraction, and rewriting of memories.

### 9. Elation (欢愉)

- **Aeon**: Aha (Aeon of Elation / Lord of the Masked Fools)
- **Core ideal**: joy, mischief, change, surprise
- **Aeon status**: alive
- **Representative characters**: Aventurine, Jade, Sparkle (?)
- **Combat role**: special mechanics / high randomness
- **Lore notes**: Aha is the most inscrutable Aeon, dedicated to "finding amusement in the universe," and founded the "Masked Fools" organization. Elation Pathstriders' abilities usually carry randomness and change, making them hard to predict.

### 10. Propagation (繁育)

- **Aeon**: Tayzzyronth (the Swarm Emperor; Aeon of Propagation — since slain)
- **Core ideal**: reproduction, proliferation, evolution, devouring
- **Aeon status**: dead (hunted down by a coalition of Aeons)
- **Representative characters**: none (the Path died out along with its Aeon)
- **Lore notes**: The Swarm Emperor's power of Propagation triggered the "Swarm Disaster," threatening the entire universe, and was finally hunted down by a coalition of Aeons. The Propagation Path died out with its Aeon, but the legacy left behind (such as the "Power of Propagation") still circulates in the universe.

### 11. Permanence (不朽) (Remembrance? / Permanence)

- **Aeon**: Long (Aeon of Permanence; dead or sleeping)
- **Core ideal**: eternity, immutability, survival, reincarnation
- **Aeon status**: dead / sleeping (exact status pending confirmation)
- **Representative characters**: Imbibitor Lunae (the Dragon Lord, leader of the Vidyadhara)
- **Lore notes**: The Permanence Aeon "Long" is the guardian deity of the Vidyadhara; after Long's death / slumber, the Vidyadhara pass down its power through the Dragon Lord. The Permanence Path is closely tied to the Vidyadhara's reincarnation mechanism.

### 12. Order (秩序)

- **Aeon**: Ena (Aeon of Order; since absorbed)
- **Core ideal**: order, rules, unity, law
- **Aeon status**: dead (absorbed by Idrila, the Harmony Aeon)
- **Representative characters**: none (the Path has merged into Harmony)
- **Lore notes**: Ena's Path of Order was absorbed by Idrila and became part of the Harmony Path. The ideals of Order (rules, unity) were incorporated into the harmony of Harmony.

### 13. Other Paths

- **Beauty (纯美)**: another title of Idrila — the same Aeon as Harmony, emphasizing the ideal of "beauty."
- **Enigmata (神秘)**: Mythus (?), pending official confirmation.
- **Equilibrium (均衡)**: Hu (互, ?), pending official confirmation.
- More Paths and Aeons may be revealed with future version updates.

## III. Paths and Characters

### Path Switching

- **The Trailblazer (protagonist)**: can unlock and switch among multiple Paths through the story (Preservation / Destruction / Harmony / Nihility / Remembrance, etc.) — the only character who can freely switch Paths.
- **Special characters**: some characters have dual-Path traits or can switch Paths under specific conditions (e.g., Dan Heng → Dan Heng • Imbibitor Lunae; Firefly's special mechanics).

### Paths and Combat Roles

| Path | Core Role | Common Mechanics |
|---|---|---|
| Preservation | Tank / shields | shields, damage reduction, taunt |
| Destruction | Offense-defense hybrid DPS | trade HP for damage, counter, lifesteal |
| Erudition | AoE DPS | bounce, blast, multi-target damage |
| Harmony | Support / buffs | ATK buff, speed buff, action advance, DMG buff |
| Nihility | Debuff / control | DEF shred, RES shred, crowd control, DoT |
| Abundance | Healing / sustain | healing, revival, continuous recovery |
| The Hunt | Single-target burst | enhanced basic attack, follow-up, high burst |
| Remembrance | Special mechanics | memory manipulation, special resources |
| Elation | Random / change | high randomness, form changes |
