# Story Timeline

> Archive of story nodes and main quests across Honkai: Star Rail versions
> Status: v0.1 (2026-08-29 initial framework, covering v1.0~4.2)
> Sources: official version update notes, Moegirlpedia, HoYoLAB Wiki

## Reading Guide

- **Main quests** (Trailblaze Missions): quests that drive the core story; quest names are marked.
- **Trailblaze Continuance**: additional story after the main quests, usually focusing on the follow-up developments of specific characters or planets.
- **Final Missions**: the concluding missions of a specific planet's arc.
- The story summaries record only the core nodes to avoid excessive spoilers; for detailed story, see in-game missions or character stories.

---

## I. Herta Space Station Arc (v1.0 Prologue)

### 1.0 "The Rail Unto the Stars"
- **Release date**: 2023-04-26
- **Main quest**: "The Rail Unto the Stars"
- **Story summary**:
  - The Stellaron Hunters Kafka and Silver Wolf infiltrated Herta Space Station, implanted the Stellaron, and awakened the Trailblazer.
  - The Trailblazer joined the Astral Express and departed the space station with Himeko, Welt, March 7th, and Dan Heng.
  - The Antimatter Legion invaded the space station; the crew defeated a Doomsday Beast during their evacuation.
- **New characters**: the Trailblazer (protagonist), Himeko, Welt, March 7th, Dan Heng, Herta, Asta, Arlan
- **New scenes**: Herta Space Station (Master Control Zone, Base Zone, Storage Zone, Supply Zone)
- **New gameplay**: basic combat system, Simulated Universe (initial version)

## II. Jarilo-VI Arc (v1.0~1.1 main story + v1.4 Continuance)

### 1.0 Jarilo-VI (Part 1)
- **Main quest**: "A Winter's Tale" and others
- **Story summary**:
  - The crew arrived at Jarilo-VI, shrouded in eternal winter, and entered the last city, Belobog.
  - Met Seele of the Wildfire organization and learned of the conflict between the upper and lower city.
  - Discovered that the Stellaron was the source of the eternal winter, and that the Supreme Guardian Cocolia had been beguiled by it.

### 1.1 "Galactic Wanderer"
- **Release date**: 2023-06-07
- **Main quest**: "In the Charcoal of Purgatory" and others (Jarilo-VI Part 2)
- **Story summary**:
  - The crew cooperated with Wildfire, stormed the heart of Belobog, and defeated Cocolia, mutated by the Stellaron.
  - Bronya became the new Supreme Guardian, ending the eternal winter and promoting reconciliation between the upper and lower city.
  - The crew departed Jarilo-VI for the Xianzhou Luofu.
- **New characters**: Yanqing (first appearance), Sushang (first appearance)
- **New scenes**: Belobog Museum of History and Culture
- **New gameplay**: Forgotten Hall (Memory of Chaos)

### 1.4 "Jolted Awake From a Winter Dream" (Trailblaze Continuance)
- **Release date**: 2023-10-11
- **Continuance quest**: "Jolted Awake From a Winter Dream"
- **Story summary**:
  - The crew returned to Jarilo-VI to deal with the Interastral Peace Corporation's debt collection.
  - Topaz and Numby appeared, representing the IPC in collecting Belobog's historical debts.
  - Bronya negotiated with Topaz and reached an agreement: Belobog would repay the debts on its own terms.
- **New characters**: Topaz & Numby
- **New scenes**: Belobog (new area)

## III. Xianzhou Luofu Arc (v1.2~1.3 main story + v1.5/2.4~2.5 Continuance)

### 1.2 "Even Immortality Ends"
- **Release date**: 2023-07-19
- **Main quest**: "Clouds Leave No Trace"
- **Story summary**:
  - The crew arrived at the Xianzhou Luofu, caught up in the Stellaron crisis and the outbreak of Abundance monstrosities.
  - Met Xianzhou figures such as Jing Yuan, Fu Xuan, and Bailu; learned of the feud between the Xianzhou Alliance and the Abundance Aeon.
  - Dan Heng's past was gradually revealed; his grudges with Blade and Jingliu surfaced.
  - The Disciples of Sanctus Medicus seized the opportunity to cause chaos, attempting to unleash the Ambrosial Arbor's Abundance power.
- **New characters**: Jing Yuan, Fu Xuan, Bailu, Blade, Kafka (story appearance), Silver Wolf (story appearance)
- **New scenes**: the Xianzhou Luofu (Exalting Sanctum, Changle Terrace, the Divination Commission, the Alchemy Commission, Scalegorge Waterscape)

### 1.3 "Celestial Eyes Above Mortal Ruins"
- **Release date**: 2023-08-30
- **Main quest**: "The Tribulations End, the War Clouds Disperse"
- **Story summary**:
  - Dan Heng awakened the power of Imbibitor Lunae, helping the crew defeat Phantylia, the leader of the Abundance monstrosities.
  - The Ambrosial Arbor was temporarily sealed, and the Stellaron crisis was brought under control (though the Stellaron remains within the Luofu).
  - Tingyun's "death" and identity mystery left foreshadowing.
  - The crew departed the Luofu and continued their journey of Trailblaze.
- **New characters**: Dan Heng • Imbibitor Lunae (playable form), Lynx
- **New scenes**: Aurum Alley
- **New gameplay**: Simulated Universe: Swarm Disaster

### 1.5 "A Foxian Tale of the Haunted" (Trailblaze Continuance)
- **Release date**: 2023-11-15
- **Continuance quest**: "A Foxian Tale of the Haunted"
- **Story summary**:
  - The crew returned to the Luofu, caught up in the supernatural incident at Fyxestroll Garden.
  - Huohuo appeared and resolved the Heliobus (evil spirit) crisis together with the crew.
  - Gained deeper insight into the Xianzhou's supernatural culture and the Ten-Lords Commission settings.
- **New characters**: Huohuo
- **New scenes**: Fyxestroll Garden

### 2.4 "Finest Duel Under the Pristine Blue (I)" (Trailblaze Continuance)
- **Release date**: 2024-07-31
- **Continuance quest**: "Finest Duel Under the Pristine Blue (I)"
- **Story summary**:
  - The Xianzhou hosted the Martial Tournament, with various factions gathering at the Luofu.
  - New characters such as Yunli and Jiaoqiu appeared, caught up in a conspiracy surrounding the tournament.
  - A felon escaped from the Shackling Prison, triggering a new crisis.
- **New characters**: Yunli, Jiaoqiu, Moze
- **New scenes**: the Shackling Prison

### 2.5 "Jade Plumes Shooting the Heavenly Wolf" (Trailblaze Continuance)
- **Release date**: 2024-09-10
- **Continuance quest**: "Finest Duel Under the Pristine Blue (II)"
- **Story summary**:
  - The conspiracy behind the Martial Tournament was exposed, and the mastermind surfaced.
  - Feixiao appeared and resolved the crisis together with the crew.
  - The Xianzhou Alliance's internal conflicts and future direction were foreshadowed.
- **New characters**: Feixiao
- **New scenes**: the Contest Ship (竞锋舰)

### 1.6 "Crown of the Mundane and Divine" (Trailblaze Continuance)
- **Release date**: 2023-12-27

## V. Penacony Arc (v2.0~2.3 main story + v2.6/2.7/3.8 Continuance/Finale)

### 2.0 "If One Dreams at Midnight"
- **Release date**: 2024-02-06
- **Main quest**: "If One Dreams at Midnight"
- **Story summary**:
  - The crew arrived at Penacony, a dreamscape planet known as the "paradise of pleasure."
  - Met characters such as Robin, Sunday, Sparkle, and Black Swan; learned of the Family's rule.
  - Discovered the dark truths behind Penacony's surface prosperity; the boundary between dream and reality began to blur.
  - Robin was stabbed at the Charmony Festival, and the story took a sharp turn.
- **New characters**: Robin, Sunday, Sparkle, Black Swan, Misha, Gallagher
- **New scenes**: Penacony (Dreamscape: the City of Morning Dew, the City of Noontide, etc.; Reality: the Scorchsand Wastes)
- **New gameplay**: Triple Path System (the protagonist can switch Paths), Divergent Universe

### 2.1 "Into the Yawning Chasm"
- **Release date**: 2024-03-27
- **Main quest**: "The Cat Among the Pigeons"
- **Story summary**:
  - After Robin's stabbing, the crew delved into investigating the truth of Penacony.
  - Acheron appeared; her true identity and past were gradually revealed.
  - Firefly's true identity (SAM) was exposed, changing her relationship with the Trailblazer.
  - The Family's Eight Lawkeepers and Penacony's history were further showcased.
- **New characters**: Acheron, Firefly (SAM), Boothill
- **New scenes**: Dewlight Pavilion, Clock Studios Theme Park
- **New gameplay**: Pathstrider Ensemble (multi-perspective switching system)

### 2.2 "Then Wake to Weep"
- **Release date**: 2024-05-08
- **Main quest**: "In Our Time"
- **Story summary**:
  - The truth of Penacony was fully revealed; the Family's dark history and its connection to the Stellaron surfaced.
  - Firefly's (SAM) sacrifice and revival became the climax of the story; the Trailblazer's romance reached its peak.
  - Acheron's past and her connection to the Stellaron Hunters were explained.
  - Penacony's fate faced a choice; the conflict between dream and reality reached its climax.
- **New characters**: (no new 5-star; focus on story progression)
- **New scenes**: Dreamflux Reef, SoulGlad™ Scorchsand Audition Venue, Penacony Grand Theater

### 2.3 "Farewell, Penacony" (main story epilogue)
- **Release date**: 2024-06-19
- **Main quest**: "Farewell, Penacony"
- **Story summary**:
  - The emotional wrap-up of the Penacony arc; the crew bid farewell to Penacony's characters.
  - Robin's fate, Firefly's whereabouts, and Sunday's choice were resolved.
  - The crew departed Penacony for the next world.
- **New characters**: Argenti (story appearance)
- **New scenes**: (no major new scenes)

### 2.6 "The Latter-Day Chronicle of Pinakonnio" (Trailblaze Continuance)
- **Release date**: 2024-10-23
- **Continuance quest**: "The Unrighteous Banana Way • The War of No Mercy"
- **Story summary**:
  - Returned to Penacony, caught up in an incident at Paperfold University College.
  - Jade appeared; the internal struggles of the Interastral Peace Corporation were showcased.
  - Penacony's subsequent developments and the remnants of the Family were addressed.
- **New characters**: Jade
- **New scenes**: Paperfold University College
- **New gameplay**: Simulated Universe: Unknowable Domain

### 2.7 "Departure on the Eighth Day" (Trailblaze Mission)
- **Release date**: 2024-12-04
- **Main quest**: "Departure on the Eighth Day"
- **Story summary**:
  - The true finale of the Penacony arc, reviewing the journey across the entire dreamscape planet.
  - The crew's party car appeared; the characters' emotions were summarized.
  - Foreshadowing for the journey to Amphoreus.
- **New characters**: (no new 5-star; focus on emotional wrap-up)
- **New scenes**: Astral Express - party car

### 3.8 "Memory Is the Prologue of Dreams" (Final Mission)
- **Release date**: 2025-12-17
- **Final mission**: "Memory Is the Prologue of Dreams"
- **Story summary**:
  - The final wrap-up of the Penacony arc, delving into the Remembrance Path settings.
  - The Oak Canker Dream appeared, uncovering Penacony's last secrets.
  - Robin's and Penacony's fates received their final resolution.
- **New characters**: (pending confirmation)
- **New scenes**: Penacony - the Oak Canker Dream (橡木鸣蛀之梦)

## VI. Amphoreus Arc (v3.0~3.7 main story)

### 3.0 "Paean of Era Nova"
- **Release date**: 2025-01-15
- **Main quest**: "Heroic Annals of the Flame-Chase"
- **Story summary**:
  - The crew arrived at Amphoreus, a world abandoned by the gods.
  - Met Golden-Blooded characters such as Aglaea, Tribbie, and Cyrene; learned of Amphoreus's mythological system.
  - The new Path "Remembrance" was implemented; the Trailblazer can switch to the Remembrance Path.
  - Amphoreus's creation myth and the background of the Flame-Chase journey were showcased.
- **New characters**: Aglaea, Tribbie, Cyrene, The Herta, Mydei, Chang Geng (长庚)
- **New scenes**: Amphoreus (initial area)
- **New gameplay**: the Remembrance Path, new combat mechanics

### 3.1 "The Gates Open, the Thrones End"
- **Release date**: 2025-02-26
- **Main quest**: "The Gates Open, the Thrones End"
- **Story summary**:
  - Delved into Amphoreus's core areas, reaching the Half-God Senate and the city-states.
  - The internal conflicts of the Golden-Blooded and Amphoreus's ruling system were showcased.
  - The truth of the Flame-Chase gradually emerged; the identity of the Flame Reaver left foreshadowing.
- **New characters**: Nakexia (那刻夏), Kelvdra (刻律德菈)
- **New scenes**: the Grove of Epiphany (神悟树庭), Janusopolis (雅努萨波利斯)

### 3.2 "Through the Flowers of the Sleeping Land"
- **Release date**: 2025-04-09
- **Main quest**: "Through the Flowers of the Sleeping Land"
- **Story summary**:
  - Explored Amphoreus's ancient ruins; learned of the history of the Dragon-Skeleton Ancient City and the Half-God Senate.
  - Castorice appeared; her connection to the Remembrance Path was showcased.
  - More of Amphoreus's creation myth and the secrets of the Titans were revealed.
- **New characters**: Castorice, Changyeyue (长夜月)
- **New scenes**: the Half-God Senate (Dawn Cloud Cliffs) (半神议院·黎明云崖), the Dragon-Skeleton Ancient City (Styxia) (龙骸古城·斯缇科西亚)

### 3.3 "Falling When Dawn Rises"
- **Release date**: 2025-05-21
- **Main quest**: "Falling When Dawn Rises"
- **Story summary**:
  - Amphoreus's crisis broke out in full; the legacy of the gods clashed with the fate of mortals.
  - Explored key areas such as the Dome Pass, the Cloud Remnant Fortress, and the Sunless Prayer Hall.
  - The split among the Golden-Blooded and Amphoreus's future direction became the focus.
- **New characters**: Phainon (白厄)
- **New scenes**: the Dome Pass (Eye of Dusk and Dawn) (穹顶关塞·晨昏之眼), the Cloud Remnant Fortress (Eye of Dusk and Dawn) (云端遗堡·晨昏之眼), the Sunless Prayer Hall (Dawn Cloud Cliffs) (无晖祈堂·黎明云崖), the Sunken Dusk City (Okhema) (沉沦暮城·奥赫玛)

### 3.4 "Because the Sun Shall Be Destroyed"
- **Release date**: 2025-07-02
- **Main quest**: "Because the Sun Shall Be Destroyed"
- **Story summary**:
  - The Flame Reaver's true identity was revealed; the end of the Flame-Chase was uncovered.
  - Amphoreus's creation truth and destruction crisis were fully showcased.
  - Phainon's character and the secret of the Genesis Vortex became the core of the story.
  - The Fate/stay night [Unlimited Blade Works] collab event "A Dream of Beauty and the Holy Grail" (美梦与圣杯) ran concurrently.
- **New characters**: Saifeier (赛飞儿)
- **New scenes**: the Secret Pavilion of Sorrow (哀丽秘榭)
- **Collab characters**: Saber, Archer, Rin Tohsaka, Gilgamesh

### 3.5 "Before the Hero Dies"
- **Release date**: 2025-08-11
- **Main quest**: "Before the Hero Dies"
- **Story summary**:
  - Amphoreus's heroic narrative reached its climax; mortals challenged the legacy of the gods.
  - Delved into Styxia to learn the final secrets of the Dragon-Skeleton Ancient City.
  - The Golden-Blooded and mortals faced their final choices.
- **New characters**: Busitu (不死途)
- **New scenes**: Styxia (new area) (斯缇科西亚)

### 3.6 "Returning to the Earth From the Long Night"
- **Release date**: 2025-09-24
- **Main quest**: "Returning to the Earth From the Long Night"
- **Story summary**:
  - A turning point in the Amphoreus arc, symbolizing the return to the earth from the long night.
  - Amphoreus's fate received a phase resolution, laying foreshadowing for what follows.
- **New characters**: (pending confirmation)
- **New gameplay**: Apocalyptic Shadow

### 3.7 "Become the Tomorrow of Yesterday"
- **Release date**: 2025-11-05
- **Main quest**: "Become the Tomorrow of Yesterday"
- **Story summary**:
  - Deeper into the Amphoreus arc; explored the Tomb of the Nameless Titan and the Ruins of Time.
  - Amphoreus's past and future intertwined; the Remembrance Path settings were deepened.
- **New characters**: (pending confirmation)
- **New scenes**: the Lingering Warmth of the Disaster Dream (Tomb of the Nameless Titan) (灾梦余温·无名泰坦大墓), the Far Shore of Buried Memories (Ruins of Time) (葬忆彼岸·时光归墟)
- **New gameplay**: Aetherium Wars

### 4.0 "The Full Moon Is the Time When Gods Are Absent"
- **Release date**: 2026-02-13
