# Planets and Locations

> Setting document for the major planets and locations of Honkai: Star Rail
> Status: v0.1 (2026-08-29 initial framework)
> Sources: in-game story, official settings, HoYoLAB Wiki

## I. Herta Space Station (黑塔空间站)

### Overview

- **Location**: an independent space station deep in the cosmos
- **Administrator**: Herta (Genius Society #83)
- **Associated faction**: the Intelligentsia Guild
- **First appeared**: v1.0 (the game's starting point)

### Features

- An important research base of the Intelligentsia Guild, housing a large collection of cosmic wonders, artifacts, and research results.
- Composed of multiple zones: Master Control Zone, Base Zone, Storage Zone, Supply Zone, etc.
- The station contains extensive Simulated Universe research equipment; Herta develops the Simulated Universe here.
- The starting point of the Astral Express story, where the Trailblazer was awakened after Kafka and Silver Wolf implanted a Stellaron.

### Key Figures

- **Herta**: owner of the space station, a genius scientist who operates through puppet bodies.
- **Asta**: station manager, in charge of daily operations.
- **Arlan**: head of station security, responsible for defending the station.
- **Pela**: (temporarily stationed) an old acquaintance of Silver Wolf, responsible for intelligence analysis.

### Stellaron Crisis

- The Antimatter Legion invaded the space station, attempting to seize the Stellaron held in containment.
- The Trailblazer was awakened here and joined the Astral Express, beginning the journey of Trailblaze.

## II. Jarilo-VI (雅利洛-VI)

### Overview

- **Location**: an ice planet shrouded in an eternal winter
- **Main city**: Belobog (贝洛伯格)
- **Associated factions**: the Supreme Guardian of Belobog, the Silvermane Guards, Wildfire
- **First appeared**: v1.0~1.2

### Features

- The planet's surface is covered by an eternal winter; humanity lives in the last city, Belobog.
- The city is divided into two layers: the upper (administrative / commercial districts) and the lower (underground mining).
- The climate is extremely cold and the outside environment is uninhabitable; humans rely on the city's energy barrier to survive.
- The descent of a Stellaron triggered the "eternal winter," plunging the planet into ice.

### Structure of Belobog

#### Upper City
- **Administrative District**: seat of the Supreme Guardian and the highest council; the city's political center.
- **Commercial District**: the area concentrated with shops, restaurants, and entertainment venues.
- **Silvermane Guards HQ**: base of the city's military force.

#### Lower City
- **Underground Mines**: source of the city's energy; miners extract ore here.
- **Wildfire Base**: stronghold of the underground resistance organization "Wildfire."
- **Rivet Town**: an abandoned town in the lower city, occupied by mutated creatures.

### Key Figures

- **Bronya**: heir / new Supreme Guardian of Belobog, commander of the Silvermane Guards.
- **Seele**: member of the underground resistance organization "Wildfire," forthright by nature.
- **Gepard**: commander of the Silvermane Guards, in charge of the city's defense.
- **Pela**: intelligence officer of the Silvermane Guards, Gepard's subordinate.
- **Cocolia**: former Supreme Guardian, influenced by the Stellaron, became a boss.
- **Oleg**: leader of Wildfire, a former member of the Silvermane Guards.
- **Hook**: member of Wildfire, a lively little girl.

### Stellaron Crisis

- After the Stellaron descended and triggered the eternal winter, Cocolia was beguiled by the Stellaron and tried to fuse with it.
- The Astral Express cooperated with Wildfire, defeated Cocolia, separated the Stellaron, and ended the eternal winter.
- Bronya became the new Supreme Guardian, leading Belobog toward rebirth.

## III. The Xianzhou Luofu (仙舟罗浮)

### Overview

- **Location**: one of the six Xianzhou ships; the core of the Xianzhou Alliance
- **Type**: a giant vessel / mobile city sailing through the sea of stars
- **Associated factions**: the Xianzhou Alliance, the Cloud Knights, the Vidyadhara, the Ten-Lords Commission
- **First appeared**: v1.0~1.6 (main story), with continued appearances afterward

### Features

- The core ship of the Xianzhou Alliance, with a long history and formidable military power.
- Blends Eastern xianxia style with sci-fi elements; a representative of "space xianxia."
- The inhabitants are mainly long-lived species, including humans, the Vidyadhara, and the Foxians.
- The Xianzhou people live long thanks to the Abundance blessing, but also face the curse of "Mara-Struck."

### Main Areas

#### 1. Exalting Sanctum (星槎海中枢)
- The commercial / transport hub of the Xianzhou Luofu; docking port for star skiffs.
- Concentrated with shops, restaurants, and inns; the most bustling area.

#### 2. Changle Terrace (长乐天)
- The cultural / entertainment center of the ship, with stages, teahouses, and markets.
- The Foxians' main activity area.

#### 3. The Divination Commission (太卜司)
- The Xianzhou's divination / prediction institution, responsible for predicting the future and celestial signs.
- Seat of Fu Xuan (head of the Divination Commission).

#### 4. The Artisanship Commission (工造司)
- The Xianzhou's industrial / forging center, responsible for manufacturing weapons and equipment.
- Closely connected to the Xianzhou Zhuming (the industrial ship).

#### 5. Scalegorge Waterscape (鳞渊境)
- The holy land of the Vidyadhara and residence of the Dragon Lord.
- Dan Heng's (Imbibitor Lunae) homeland, with ancient draconic ruins.

#### 6. The Shackling Prison (幽囚狱)
- The Xianzhou's prison, holding felons and dangerous individuals.
- Blade was once imprisoned here.

#### 7. The Ambrosial Arbor (建木)
- The sacred tree of the Xianzhou, a product of the Abundance Aeon Yaoshi's blessing.
- The core of the Stellaron crisis; after being eroded by Abundance power, it spawned "Abundance monstrosities."

### Key Figures

- **Jing Yuan**: General of the Xianzhou Luofu (the Divine Foresight), supreme commander of the Cloud Knights.
- **Fu Xuan**: head of the Divination Commission, in charge of divination and prediction; later becomes General of the Luofu.
- **Yanqing**: Cloud Knight lieutenant, Jing Yuan's attendant, a genius swordsman.
- **Bailu**: a Vidyadhara dragon girl with healing abilities; mysterious identity.
- **Tingyun**: a Foxian emissary, responsible for diplomacy and liaison.
- **Yukong**: Helmsmaster of the Sky-Faring Commission, in charge of star skiff dispatch.
- **Luocha**: a mysterious golden-haired merchant with powerful healing abilities, connected to Cocolia.
- **Dan Heng / Dan Heng • Imbibitor Lunae**: reincarnation of the Vidyadhara Dragon Lord, a former Luofu criminal, now a member of the Astral Express.
- **Blade**: member of the Stellaron Hunters, former Sword Champion of the Luofu, Mara-Struck.
- **Jingliu**: former Sword Champion of the Luofu, Blade's master, eroded by Mara-Struck.
- **Xueyi / Hanya**: Ten-Lords judges, responsible for judging departed souls.

### Stellaron Crisis

- A Stellaron descended upon the Luofu and fused with the Ambrosial Arbor, triggering an outbreak of Abundance monstrosities.
- The Disciples of Sanctus Medicus (an underground organization worshipping Yaoshi) seized the opportunity to cause chaos, attempting to unleash the Arbor's power.
- The Astral Express cooperated with the Cloud Knights, defeated the Abundance monstrosities and the Disciples of Sanctus Medicus, and temporarily brought the Stellaron crisis under control.
- Dan Heng awakened the power of Imbibitor Lunae here, helping resolve the crisis.
- The Stellaron still resides within the Luofu; it will need to be dealt with later.

## IV. Penacony (匹诺康尼)

### Overview

- **Location**: formerly an Interastral Peace Corporation prison planet, now a "paradise of pleasure"
- **Type**: a dreamscape planet / entertainment resort
- **Associated factions**: the Family (the Eight Lawkeepers), the Interastral Peace Corporation, the Memokeepers, the Masked Fools
- **First appeared**: v2.0~2.3

### Features

- Once an IPC prison planet, later taken over by "the Family" and transformed into a dream world devoted to the pursuit of pleasure.
- The planet's reality and dreams are interwoven; residents enjoy eternal happiness within dreams.
- On the surface it is prosperous and joyful, but dark secrets and power struggles lurk beneath.
- Closely tied to the Remembrance Path and the Memokeepers.

### Main Areas

#### 1. The Dreamscape (梦境)
- Penacony's main activity area, where residents live within dreams.
- Divided into several "moments" (the City of Morning Dew (朝露之都), the City of Noontide (正午之都), the City of Dusk (黄昏之都), the City of Long Night (长夜之都)) — official English names pending.
- The rules of dreams differ from reality, with special physical laws.

#### 2. Reality (现实)
- Penacony's true surface — desolate and ruined.
- Called the "Scorchsand Wastes" (焚风热土, lit. "fiery-wind hot lands"), an area abandoned by the Family.
- Wanderers and rebels survive here.

#### 3. Golden Hour (黄金的时刻)
- Penacony's most prosperous area and the center of the Family's power.
- Grand theaters, casinos, and luxury hotels are concentrated here.

### Key Figures

- **Robin**: lead singer of the "Charmony Festival" (谐乐大典) in Penacony, a singer on the Harmony Path.
- **Sunday**: Robin's brother, the Family's "observer," Penacony's de facto administrator.
- **Firefly**: Penacony's "SAM" (member of the Antimatter Legion), active in Penacony under a hidden identity.
- **Aventurine**: head of the IPC Strategic Partnership Department, one of the Ten Stonehearts.
- **Jade**: head of the IPC Strategic Investment Department, one of the Ten Stonehearts.
- **Sparkle**: member of the Masked Fools, causing chaos in Penacony.
- **The Herta**: Genius Society member, active in Penacony in her adult form.
- **Acheron**: a mysterious swordsman connected to Penacony's past.

### Stellaron Crisis

- Penacony itself is a giant "Stellaron" (or deeply affected by one).
- The Family controls the residents through dreams, concealing the planet's dark truths.
- The Astral Express became embroiled in Penacony's power struggles, uncovering the Family's secrets.
- Robin's "death" and revival, Firefly's true identity, and Acheron's past are the core mysteries of the story.

## V. Amphoreus (翁法罗斯) (Eris / related planet)

### Overview

- **Location**: a new planet in v3.0
- **Type**: a world abandoned by the gods / a myth-styled planet
- **Associated factions**: the city-states, the gods, the Golden-Blooded
- **First appeared**: v3.0~

### Features

- The main stage of v3.0, with a unique mythological / fantasy style.
- The planet was abandoned by the gods; humans built city-states among the ruins, struggling to survive.
- Closely tied to new settings such as the "Golden-Blooded" and the Remembrance Path.
- Detailed settings will be added with version updates.

### Key Figures (v3.x)

- **Aglaea**: a "Golden-Blooded" of Amphoreus, a powerful warrior.
- **Tribbie**: Golden-Blooded member, lively.
- **Cyrene**: Golden-Blooded member, a mysterious singer.
- **Chang Geng (长庚)**: Golden-Blooded member with a special energy mechanic.
- **Busitu (不死途)**: Remembrance Path character, connected to Amphoreus's past.
- **Mydei (万敌)**: Destruction Path character, a warrior who burns HP for damage.
