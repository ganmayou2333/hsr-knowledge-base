# Worldview Overview

> Basic setting document for the Honkai: Star Rail worldview (Astral Express / Stellaron / Antimatter Legion / the Trailblaze mission / Aeons and Paths)
> Status: v0.1 (2026-08-29 initial framework)
> Sources: in-game story, official settings, HoYoLAB Wiki

## I. Cosmology

### Basic Settings

- **The world**: Honkai: Star Rail's story takes place in a universe made up of countless **worlds** (planets / star systems), connected to one another by **Star Rails** (interstellar routes).
- **Aeons**: the most powerful beings in the universe; each governs a **Path** (philosophy / law) and influences the universe through that Path.
- **Pathstriders**: beings who practice the ideals of a Path can gain its power; a character's "Path" is the road they walk.
- **Stellarons**: mysterious objects known as the "cancer of all worlds"; when one descends upon a world, it triggers disasters (such as a Stellaron crisis) and changes the world's fate.

### The Trailblaze Mission

- **The Astral Express**: a legacy left behind by the late Aeon of Trailblaze, Akivili — a miraculous train capable of traversing Star Rails and traveling between worlds.
- **The Trailblaze mission**: the crew of the Astral Express (the Trailblazer / the Express crew) take "Trailblaze" as their mission — to journey to worlds affected by Stellarons, resolve Stellaron crises, and explore the unknown.
- **The Express crew**:
  - **Himeko**: the train's navigator / restorer, and its de facto leader.
  - **Welt Yang**: a former "First Herrscher" from another world, the train's guide and combatant.
  - **March 7th**: a mysterious girl frozen within Six-Phase Ice, a member of the Express crew.
  - **Dan Heng**: a mysterious young man burdened by his past, a member of the Express crew (his true identity is the reincarnation of the Dragon Lord, Imbibitor Lunae).
  - **The Trailblazer (protagonist)**: a special being implanted with a Stellaron, controlled by the player, able to switch among Preservation / Destruction / Harmony / Nihility and other Paths.

## II. Core Conflicts

### The Antimatter Legion

- **The Antimatter Legion**: an invading force led by Nanook, the Aeon of Destruction, whose goal is to "destroy all civilizations."
- **Legion structure**:
  - **Nanook**: the Aeon of Destruction, supreme ruler of the Antimatter Legion.
  - **The Stellaron Hunters**: a mysterious organization hired by / cooperating with Nanook, responsible for deploying Stellarons on various worlds and triggering disasters.
  - **Antimatter soldiers**: the Legion's basic fighting force, including Voidrangers (虚卒), marauders (溃兵), and others.
- **Goal**: Nanook considers civilizations the "cancer" of the universe and works to trigger each world's self-destruction through Stellarons, ultimately returning the entire universe to nihility.

### The Stellaron Hunters

- **Role**: a mysterious organization active across worlds, hired by Nanook (or other factions) to deploy Stellarons and steer fate.
- **Known members**:
  - **Kafka**: a core member of the Stellaron Hunters with mental-control abilities; composed and elegant.
  - **Silver Wolf**: a hacker / augmented human among the Stellaron Hunters, skilled at treating reality like a game she can modify; easygoing by nature.
  - **Blade**: a swordsman in the Stellaron Hunters, cursed with "Mara-Struck," holding past grudges against Dan Heng.
  - **SAM**: a member of the Stellaron Hunters, a warrior clad in heavy armor.
  - **Elio**: the "scriptwriter" of the Stellaron Hunters, able to foresee the future and devise action scripts for the organization.
- **Relationship with the Express**: both friend and foe. The Stellaron Hunters clashed with the Express crew multiple times during the Jarilo-VI and Xianzhou Luofu incidents, but sometimes cooperate over shared interests.

## III. The Path System

### Paths and Aeons

Paths are the philosophies / laws governed by Aeons, and the source of characters' power. Known Paths include:

| Path | Aeon | Core Ideal | Representative Characters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Preservation | Qlipoth | protection, defense, bulwarks | Trailblazer (Preservation), Gepard, March 7th, Fu Xuan |
| Destruction | Nanook | destruction, ruin, finality | Trailblazer (Destruction), Arlan, Hook, Seele |
| Erudition | Nous | knowledge, wisdom, truth | Himeko, Herta, Qingque, Jing Yuan |
| Harmony | Idrila (missing) | harmony, resonance, connection | Trailblazer (Harmony), Bronya, Tingyun, Robin |
| Nihility | Oroboros (sleeping) | nihility, negation, extinction | Welt, Sampo, Pela, Silver Wolf, Kafka |
| Abundance | Yaoshi | life, healing, endlessness | Natasha, Bailu, Luocha, Huohuo |
| The Hunt | Lan | vengeance, pursuit, swiftness | Dan Heng, Seele (The Hunt), Yanqing |
| Remembrance | Focalors (?) | memory, recollection, history | Trailblazer (Remembrance), The Herta |
| Elation | Aha | joy, mischief, change | Aventurine, Jade |
| Propagation | Tayzzyronth (dead) | reproduction, proliferation, evolution | — |
| Beauty | Idrila (the Harmony Aeon, also called the Aeon of Beauty) | beauty, art, perfection | — |
| Permanence | Long (dead / sleeping) | eternity, immutability, survival | Imbibitor Lunae (the Dragon Lord) |
| Order | Ena (dead) | order, rules, unity | — |
| Enigmata | Mythus (?) | mystery, the unknown, riddles | — |
| Equilibrium | Hu (互, ?) | balance, parity, reconciliation | — |

> Note: some Aeon statuses (alive / dead / sleeping / missing) are subject to the latest official settings; the number of Paths may grow with version updates.

### Paths and Combat

- A character's Path determines their combat role (Preservation = tank, Destruction = DPS, Erudition = AoE DPS, Harmony = support, Nihility = debuffs, Abundance = healing, The Hunt = single-target DPS).
- A Path is not a class restriction: the same character can switch Paths through special mechanics (e.g., the Trailblazer can switch among multiple Paths), and some characters have dual-Path traits.
- Path-Aeon relationship: the stronger a character's practice of a Path, the closer they approach that Path Aeon's ideals; Aeons can bestow power upon Pathstriders through the Path.

## IV. Key Concepts

### Stellarons and Worlds

- **Stellaron**: known as the "cancer of all worlds"; after descending upon a world, it fuses with the world's core and triggers various disasters (e.g., Jarilo-VI's eternal winter, the Xianzhou Luofu's Abundance monstrosities).
- **Stellaron crisis**: after a Stellaron descends, a world's fate is warped, and external forces (such as the Astral Express) are needed to resolve it.
- **Resolution**: usually requires defeating the core person / creature affected by the Stellaron, or finding a way to separate the Stellaron.

### Mara-Struck

- **Mara-Struck**: a mental / physical mutation afflicting the Xianzhou natives (a long-lived species) due to their excessive lifespan; it causes people to lose their sanity and turn into monsters.
- **Blade** is Mara-Struck and relies on special means to maintain his sanity.
- One of the Xianzhou Alliance's core goals is to find a cure for Mara-Struck (related to the Abundance Aeon Yaoshi).

### The Dragon Lord and the Vidyadhara

- **The Vidyadhara**: one of the native species of the Xianzhou Luofu, bearing draconic traits and able to be reborn through reincarnation.
- **The Dragon Lord**: the supreme leader of the Vidyadhara, possessing great power; each generation's Dragon Lord is called "Imbibitor Lunae."
- **Dan Heng**'s true identity is the reincarnation of the previous Imbibitor Lunae, **Dan Feng**; he left the Xianzhou and joined the Astral Express because of the sins of his past.
