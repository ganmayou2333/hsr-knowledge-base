## 任务描述

矿区风波暂告一段落。「地火」首领奥列格为报答各位无名客的支援，他同意与你们合作，并为你们安排了合适的住处。

不过，上一次在大人物安排下入住旅店、随后被人反手捅一刀仿佛就发生在昨天。

不知道现在返回列车，向帕姆索要绊线地雷还来得来不及…

---

## 任务过程

1. Go to the accommodation arranged by Oleg
2. Have some rest at the Goethe Grand Hotel
3. Go outside and take a walk
4. Talk and walk with Bronya

#### 分支 2

#### 分支 3

**丹恒**：...

**三月七**：Don't you have something cool to say, like "Appearances can be deceiving"?

#### 分支 4

**布洛妮娅**：Not really, though it wasn't the friendliest conversation. We didn't see eye to eye on everything.

**布洛妮娅**：I've come to know a great deal in a short time — I doubt I'll be able to fall asleep tonight.

**三月七**：I doubt I'll be able to sleep either... not that I don't trust you! It's just... you're a Silvermane Guard officer in the underground. There might be people looking to harm you.

**布洛妮娅**：I wouldn't be surprised if there are... the Silvermane Guards have a lot to make amends for down here.

**布洛妮娅**：If someone had made a stand in the outset... and explained the consequences to Madam Cocolia... Forget it. It's meaningless to talk about it now.

**布洛妮娅**：You don't need to worry about me. Oleg guaranteed that I wouldn't be harmed. He seemed like a man of his word. Go and rest.

## 三、出去散散心

这一晚虽没有军靴踢踏扰人清梦，可你却被梦中诡谲异常的低语惊醒。

一连几天里，你的噩梦内容都是如此。这不寻常的巧合使你辗转难眠。

硬逼着自己睡着只会使你更加焦虑…横竖都睡不着，不如出门散散心吧。

你走出门，看到了守夜的布洛妮娅…

**布洛妮娅**：Who goes there? Show yourself!

**布洛妮娅**：...It's you. Don't sneak up behind me next time. Lucky for you I wasn't armed.

**布洛妮娅**：No sleep for you either, huh?


> **分支选项（开拓者可选择）：**
>
> 1. **最近总是噩梦缠身。**
> 2. **百分之百！**
> 3. **你也是身不由己。**
> 4. **我们会帮你。**
> 5. **这世界比你想的要复杂。**

---

#### 分支 1

**布洛妮娅**：I understand. The more that weighs on your mind, the more your fears become dreamscapes.

**布洛妮娅**：Actually, seeing as there's no one around... can I ask you some things?

**布洛妮娅**：那个所谓的「星核」（灾祸的源头）…假设真的找到了它，你们有多大把握能阻止寒潮？

---

#### 分支 2

**布洛妮娅**：Your confidence actually makes me even more worried... This isn't like helping a child make a snowball.

**布洛妮娅**：You know, it was difficult for me to believe you. Some of the words you use are too alien for those that have lived their entire life in Belobog.

**布洛妮娅**：As far as I was concerned, Madam Cocolia's order to arrest you sounded reasonable... but why didn't she issue it straight away?

**布洛妮娅**：She must have realized something about you... Something I haven't yet been able to perceive for myself, and it bothers me...

**布洛妮娅**：What must have transpired to cause her such a drastic change? ...Still, orders are orders. It's not for soldiers to question their superiors.

---

#### 分支 3

**布洛妮娅**：I don't need you to absolve me. The truth is, I didn't have every confidence that I was doing the right thing, but the sense of duty to carry out an order is bound to prevail with me.

**布洛妮娅**：In the past, Overworld soldiers on the front line would lose their lives in droves, and yet the Fragmentum's advance would remain unaffected.

**布洛妮娅**：Madam Cocolia decided on a last-ditch offensive. She thought that if the surface fell, the underground would immediately collapse without source of supply. However, in the reverse scenario, humanity would hold out longer.

**布洛妮娅**：Nevertheless, that was her reason for deploying all the Silvermane Guards to the front line. The transport line for supplies and Geomarrow remained open, but the passage between the surface and the underground ceased. I approved of that decision...

**布洛妮娅**：I never thought that the Underworld would sink into such ruin without the Silvermane Guards... Perhaps mother was wrong...

**布洛妮娅**：But how can I change her mind? I've tried, but she won't listen. I don't know how to get through to her...

---

#### 分支 4

**布洛妮娅**：Help me change the mind of the Madam Guardian? Not easy. Nobody knows that better than I do.

**布洛妮娅**：The more I think about it, the more disoriented I get, which in turn makes me think about it even more... I just want a better life for the people of Belobog...

---

#### 分支 5

**布洛妮娅**：...Maybe you're right, or not. Right now I just... need more time to think.

**布洛妮娅**：Walk with me. Let's forget our problems for a while.

## 任务总结

相会在日落时分是第一章雅利洛-VI的第九个子任务，讲述了开拓者入住歌德大饭店、梦境中看到可可利亚与星核之声的对话、与布洛妮娅深夜散步聊天、了解可可利亚封锁上下层区决策的过程。

### 关键信息

- **歌德大饭店**：下层区的旅店，地上歌德宾馆的"总店"，几百年前歌德家前辈白手起家
- **歌蒂**：歌德大饭店的老板，热情好客
- **可可利亚的梦境**：开拓者梦到可可利亚失去了女儿（可能是布洛妮娅？），星核之声承诺"新世界"和"团聚"
- **星核之声**：称可可利亚为"守护者"，要求她"完成宿命"和"完成一个母亲的愿望"
- **可可利亚的决策**：把所有银鬃铁卫调去前线，封锁上下层往来，只保留供给和地髓交换通道
- **布洛妮娅的疑惑**：曾经认同母亲的决策，但看到下层区的残败后开始怀疑，试图改变母亲的想法但失败了
- **守夜安排**：布洛妮娅主动要求守夜，奥列格保证不会伤害她

### 角色关系

- **布洛妮娅与可可利亚**：母女关系，布洛妮娅对母亲的决策有疑惑，但仍然服从命令
- **布洛妮娅与开拓者**：暂时休战合作，深夜散步聊天，逐渐建立信任
- **歌蒂与奥列格**：奥列格安排开拓者入住歌德大饭店，歌蒂热情款待

### 伏笔

- **可可利亚失去的女儿**：梦境中可可利亚说"我失去她了"，这个"她"是谁？是布洛妮娅吗？还是其他人？
- **星核之声的承诺**："你没有失去她…她在另一头等着和你团聚"，"新世界"是什么？
- **可可利亚的宿命**：星核之声要求她"完成宿命"和"完成一个母亲的愿望"，具体是什么？
- **布洛妮娅的立场转变**：她开始怀疑母亲的决策，可能会在后续剧情中选择与开拓者合作
- **上下层区封锁的真相**：可可利亚的决策可能不仅仅是为了抵御裂界，可能与星核计划有关
