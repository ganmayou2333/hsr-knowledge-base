## 一、星神的陨落

Concurrently, at the other end of the Luofu...

**素裳**：Hey, Luocha, you only told half the story about 'Propagation' (the Aeon) — keep going, it's rather interesting. How odd — Aeons can die too? Aren't they invincible?

**罗刹**：There is no true invincibility or immortality in the world — such exaggerations are born of the perspectives of ordinary beings. Nonetheless, ordinary beings could not have orchestrated the fall of the Propagation... That Aeon was slain at the hands of another Aeon.

**素裳**：...I don't understand. They're all Aeons, why do they want to fight?

**罗刹**：You... are you really from the Xianzhou?! Setting aside other Aeons, you must at least know the story of Lan (the Aeon of 'the Hunt') and Yaoshi (the Aeon of 'Abundance'), right? Isn't the Alliance's long-cherished wish to destroy the Aeon Yaoshi?

**素裳**：Of course, I know! Well, I know a little. My mum made me practice with swords all day — I didn't really go to school...

**罗刹**：In that case... let's just change the subject. If you don't know about the feud between The Hunt and the Abundance, I'm afraid there's just too much to explain.

**素裳**：Fine, how's this for a subject — what's in the box?

**罗刹**：Oh... this one? It's a casket, more commonly known as a coffin. It's for containing the remains of the deceased.

**素裳**：The deceased... A—Aren't you a merchant?

**罗刹**：Yes indeed, this is just part of my job. I was asked to deliver this coffin to the Xianzhou. Ah, I'd quite forgotten — for long-life species, death is probably a distant concept?

**素裳**：Not exactly — Cloud Knights devote themselves to the battlefield, where death is an everyday matter. But we don't have the custom of storing remains in boxes... er, coffins. The Xianzhou custom for bidding farewell to fallen comrades is to enshrine their names and jade tablets in the Hall of Karma at the 'Ten-Lords Commission' (the authority managing longevity).

**素裳**：The foxians and the Vidyadhara have their own ceremonies. Foxian soldiers place their dead in Starskiffs and then let them drift out into the stars. They call it "the Verdantia Returning."

**素裳**：As for the Vidyadhara, they're... more mysterious. They say that when a Vidyadhara is very old or has a fatal injury, they turn into an egg that looks like a pearl. When the shell breaks, they come out looking young again...

**素裳**：My mum calls the Vidyadhara "Long's Scions." When I was young, she told me stories of how the Vidyadhara could turn into dragons. I don't know if that's true...

**是真的。**

**素裳**：What do you know? Silent but deadly speaks.

**丹恒**：...Your mother is right — the Vidyadhara are 'descendants of the dragon,' that is, descendants of the Aeon 'Long' ('Permanence'). Thus, among the Vidyadhara, there were once those who could transform into dragons — though not everyone possesses such aptitude.

**丹恒**：The power was a rare inheritance, passed down only to those who could successfully complete numerous rites and challenges. For the inheritor, it was hard to say whether it was a blessing or a curse.

**罗刹**：...I've heard the story of Long the Permanence and THEIR descendants. Many myths and legends praise the Aeon for a rich and immortal life.

**罗刹**：But for some reason, the Aeon disappeared among the stars without a trace — almost as if THEY had never existed... leaving only THEIR descendants.

**丹恒**：...

**罗刹**：Every life has its limit. Even the Aeons are not truly immortal and will eventually reach the end of their lives.

**素裳**：Uh, let me ask you one more question... Do you know the person in the coffin?

**罗刹**：Yes.

**素裳**：Friend?

**罗刹**：No.

**素裳**：So, uh... a sweetheart?

**罗刹**：Haha, miss, whatever gave you that impression? The individual in the coffin is neither friend nor relative — we met only once. By chance, I made someone a promise, and so I have to run this errand.

**罗刹**：Let's leave it at that, shall we? I think we've all had enough rest.

## Quest Summary

诸天无安，迷途难返是第二章仙舟罗浮的第九个子任务，是丹恒视角的任务。讲述了素裳和罗刹讨论星神的陨落、罗刹的灵柩、仙舟各族的告别仪式（十王司、狐族、持明）、丹恒确认持明是"龙裔"（星神"不朽"的后代）、持明族能化龙但不是人人都有资质、三人穿过空无一人的洞天、消灭魔阴身救下受伤的少女（雪衣）、雪衣是十王司的偃偶判官（机械改造人）、罗刹用"丰饶"的力量修复了雪衣、雪衣透露她来捉拿星核猎手（刃）、雪衣带丹恒等人查看奇异变故的过程。

### Key Info

- **持明族**：星神"不朽"（龙）的后代，即"龙裔"，曾经有人能化龙，但不是人人都有资质
- **不朽的龙**：星神之一，拥有完满不朽的生命，但在群星间消失无踪，只留下孑遗子嗣
- **持明的轮回**：持明活得久了或受伤濒死，会化作珍珠般的蛋，然后以幼子形貌破壳新生
- **十王司**：仙舟的长生管理机构，仙舟人辞别同袍的习俗是将名字和玉兆供奉在十王司的因果殿里
- **正首青丘**：狐族战士将离世同胞安置在星槎里，任它飘向遥远的星辰
- **雪衣**：十王司的偃偶判官（机械改造人），来捉拿星核猎手（刃）
- **罗刹的能力**：罗刹能用"丰饶"的力量修复机巧偃偶，这根本不是普通医术
- **刃的特征**：剑技非凡，手持神兵，危险至极
- **灵柩**：罗刹携带的棺材，里面躺着与他非亲非故、仅有一面之缘的人

### Character Relations

- **丹恒与持明族**：丹恒了解持明族的秘密，他可能与持明族有某种联系
- **罗刹与丰饶**：罗刹拥有丰饶的力量，他的真实身份是什么？
- **雪衣与刃**：雪衣来捉拿刃，她与刃之间有什么恩怨？
- **素裳与罗刹**：素裳对罗刹很好奇，不断问他问题
- **丹恒与刃**：丹恒听到刃的名字后反应激烈，两人之间有刻骨宿怨

### Foreshadowing

- **丹恒的真实身份**：他为什么了解持明族的秘密？他与持明族有什么关系？
- **罗刹的真实身份**：他为什么拥有丰饶的力量？他携带的灵柩里是谁？
- **刃的真实身份**：他是谁？他与丹恒之间有什么刻骨宿怨？
- **雪衣的任务**：她为什么来捉拿刃？十王司与刃之间有什么恩怨？
- **奇异变故**：仙舟上发生了什么不可思议的变故？
- **不朽的龙的陨落**：祂为什么消失无踪？祂的陨落与持明族有什么关系？
- **持明化龙的力量**：这份力量如何承接？对接掌者为什么"难说是幸事"？
- **灵柩里的人**：罗刹为什么要运送这具灵柩？里面的人是谁？
