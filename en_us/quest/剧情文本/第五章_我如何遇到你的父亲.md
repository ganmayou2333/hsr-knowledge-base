## Story Recap

After resolving the crisis surrounding Fulwish, we returned to the Astral Express to gather our thoughts. Shuhu, Asat Pramad, and the Voracity... unforeseen challenges arrived in rapid succession. After careful deliberation, we decided to split into teams and tackle the problems in Planarcadia together.

There, Yao Guang, Dan Heng, and Welt traversed every corner of Planarcadia to deploy the "Deca-Light Reflection Barrier" as a safeguard against a potential ambush by the Lord Ravager. Meanwhile, Blade decided to set out for the edge of the World in Canvas, where he intended to use the power of the Voracity to bring his conflict with Shuhu to its final conclusion.

Elsewhere, Ashveil, March 7th and the others headed to the tavern in search of clues regarding Asat Pramad. Their investigation eventually centered on "Enroute." This former Masked Fool left the tavern for the Astral Express, becoming the Navigator known as "Isee". And the Voracity shadow of Ashveil is, in fact, his "legacy."

In addition, Ruan Mei brought word that the Phantasmoon Games were more than a game. They were a ritual enacted by Aha to suppress the dead husk of the Voracity. She further suggested that the Path of Elation may have originated from a concept stripped away from the Voracity: "Aftertaste."

At the same time, I ventured into the World in Canvas in search of Graphia at Evanescia's request. By a twist of fate, I truly did encounter the Mother of Imagenesis. She spoke of the Cindearth Age, of the day the Express fell, and of her stand against {M#Asat}{F#the Asat} Pramad, before beckoning me further into the World in Canvas.

Elsewhere, Himeko returned to Graphia Academy to accompany Ryusuke, probing him for any ties to the Destruction. As they revisited their shared memories, Ryusuke posed a chilling theory: That the real Himeko had long ago succumbed to the Weathering Curse, and the person standing before him was merely an imagenae she had painted to carry on her dream of Trailblazing.

On the banks of the "Inkford Hermitage," I crossed paths with Silver Wolf and Blade once more. There, I summoned Graphia, who ferried us into the depths of the World in Canvas. In the Meadow on the Distant Shore, Blade bid us farewell and threw himself resolutely into the Voracity's maw. Embracing the path he had walked, he reclaimed his heart as a craftsman. By harnessing the Voracity's furnace-like destructive power and the regenerative flesh of the Abundance, he vowed to temper himself into the "Blade" that would imprison Shuhu for eternity.

In the Meadow on the Distant Shore, it dawned on me that the "Graphia" we saw earlier at Inkford Hermitage was an impostor. The true culprit, Asat Pramad, revealed himself, exposing that Graphia is in fact a scion of Destruction. He then broke our assault, grievously wounding Graphia, before he departed.

As we struggled through a grueling battle against the maddened Graphia, Blade returned from his nirvana. Having tempered Shuhu within himself, he joined us to turn the tide, and with his aid, Graphia was restored to her senses. She revealed that Asat Pramad could seemingly manifest in many places at once. It was exactly as she said: And indeed, at that very same time, Ashveil, Yao Guang, and the others were also attacked by Asat Pramad.

Worst of all, even Ryusuke, Himeko's father, had been Asat Pramad in disguise from the very beginning. After revealing himself, he stood before us all and declared that an unpredictable disaster was about to descend upon Planarcadia...

## Quest Summary

我如何遇到你的父亲是第五章「二相乐园」的核心任务。前情提要详细回顾了第五章的关键剧情：解决满愿危机后兵分几路（爻光丹恒瓦尔特布置十方光映法界、刃前往画中世界尽头借贪饕力量与倏忽做个了断、不死途三月七前往酒馆寻找归寂线索疑点锁定在未抵身上、阮梅带来情报幻月游戏是阿哈镇压贪饕死躯的仪式欢愉命途或许起源于贪饕被剥离的概念回味）、开拓者按绯英委托前往画中世界寻找绘世（绘世吐露烬土纪元目睹列车坠毁对抗归寂的往事）、姬子重返绘世学院与隆介同行（隆介猜测真正的姬子早已因风化诅咒死去眼前之人不过是姬子画出的幻造生命）、渡画泉隐河畔与银狼刃再度相逢（彼岸花原刃告别决然跃入贪饕口中借助贪饕熔炉一般的破坏力与丰饶血肉的再生力誓要将自己千锤百冶为永囚倏忽之刃）、归寂出现道出绘世实为毁灭子嗣的真相（化解攻势重伤绘世并离去、与失控绘世缠斗、刃涅槃归来制胜战局、绘世指出归寂有近乎分身的能力）、最糟糕的是隆介从头到尾都是归寂假扮（撕破面具后归寂高调登场宣布自己将要让不可知的意外降临二相乐园）。任务过程讲述了与同伴们商讨对策（绝灭大君归寂已经现身尽快与同伴们会合以免被分身逐个击破、必须守住二相乐园阻止归寂释放贪饕、归寂说要去见一位真正的无名客、姬子有麻烦了、银狼说要是那个混球真的能到处复制粘贴自己要遭殃的恐怕不只有星穹列车这边、刃说兽蜕在躁动、绘世说我会为你们开辟一条从画界通往现世的道路帮助你们离开、绘世说我会留在画界中修复封印平息贪饕遗骸的蠢动、真珠说抱歉全局推演结论是您此举成功率低于3%、真珠说我观测到画中世界爆发了强烈的能量反应这才能锁定诸位的坐标、真珠说与此同时二相乐园多地也出现了类似的能量信号其本征值与公司在翁法罗斯危机期间记录的数据相符、结论绝灭大君归寂于多个位置同时现身了）的过程。
