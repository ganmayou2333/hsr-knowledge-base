## Quest Description

在与幻胧的那场大战结束后，符玄唤来星槎，将你们送回长乐天。

但关于建木升起，以及此次仙舟之行的来去始末，你们仍觉得自己身处迷局之中。为此你们来到神策府探问情况。

## 一、神策府

Head to the Seat of Divine Foresight to ask about the situation

Some time later, you arrive at the Seat of Divine Foresight to check on Jing Yuan...

**青镞**：Can you wait a little moment? The master diviner will be here soon.

**青镞**：She's temporarily taken on the general's responsibilities. Before meeting you, she must make the current situation known and fortify public spirit. This is her first real test as a general — I hope she can get used to things quickly.

**符玄**：...I don't think I can ever get used to this.

符玄从你们身后走来…

**符玄**：*sigh* It's a pleasure to see you all here today.

**符玄**：Collating casualties and losses, dispatching forces to round up the remnants of the Disciples, submitting battle reports to the Six Charioteers... Now I know what Jing Yuan meant when he said "the highest throne faces the strongest winds."

**但你很享受吧？**

**符玄**：Generalship is no trifling matter — how could I be "enjoying myself?" All I can do is get used to it, hmph...

**三月七**：(I think she's enjoying herself...)

**瓦尔特**：Master Diviner, have you summoned us here just to sigh and complain?

**符玄**：Of course not! On the contrary, I am eager to thank you all in my official capacity as acting general of the Luofu. Now that things have drawn to a conclusion, it is time to reward you.

**三月七**：Oooo! All those benefits that the general promised — they're finally here! Yippee!

**符玄**：Yes, the Astral Express has braved great evil for the Luofu. Your devotion is evident. After discussions with the Six Charioteers, you are henceforth sworn allies of the Luofu. Within the Luofu's jurisdiction, you are to be treated with the highest standard of diplomatic protocol.

**瓦尔特**：On behalf of the Astral Express, I would like to thank you, Master Diviner.

**三月七**：Wow, nothing tangible then, but at least it sounds pretty cool...

**符玄**：Now that thanks have been expressed, I still have something to discuss with you. Please, this way.

## 二、梳理建木灾厄始末

Go through the events surrounding the Ambrosial Arbor's calamity with Fu Xuan

**三月七**：...Seriously? Thanking us like that, and then immediately sending us on another mission?

**符玄**：It's not a mission. The Stellaron crisis, the Disciples of Sanctus Medicus rebellion, the Arbor's resurrection, not to mention the Antimatter Legion infiltrating the Luofu... All of these incidents need reporting at the highest level for investigation.

**符玄**：...It's just that, having been in the middle of everything, I'm struggling to keep an accurate perspective. I would appreciate if you could go through it all with me, so that I can reacquaint myself with the finer detail.

**符玄**：So, where should we begin?


> **分支选项（开拓者可选择）：**
>
> 1. **先说幻胧…**
> 2. **我们知道的情报很多了…**
> 3. **是…停云留下的东西？**

---

#### 分支 1

**符玄**：It appears that Phantylia was the mastermind behind it all. The Xianzhou warred with the Antimatter Legion in the past, and ever since we have kept a watchful eye on their movements.

**符玄**：Few could have foreseen the insidious tactics employed in the execution of their assaults. The Lord Ravager slithered in unnoticed, clandestinely plotting and machinating, and surreptitiously bestowed a Stellaron upon the Disciples of Sanctus Medicus. This heinous act instigated an uprising aimed at luring the Luofu into a perilous trap of self-destruction.

**符玄**：The present danger posed by the Legion clearly demands a re-evaluation. We should proceed cautiously.

---

#### 分支 2

**符玄**：That we may, but as logical as things might seem, there are still many details about which we're uncertain.

**符玄**：For example, how was the Stellaron brought onto the Xianzhou? And who sent it on to Scalegorge Waterscape? How many Disciples of Sanctus Medicus still remain at large? All of this is a mystery. Thus far, we've done our best.

**符玄**：I'm submitting my written report to the Alliance, and I've already got a plan in mind. All of you will feature in the report, but for the sake of Jing Yuan, anything related to Xianzhou internal affairs will have names redacted — I hope you can forgive me.

**瓦尔特**：I was about to make the same request, Master Diviner. Since the Arbor's resurrection, I'm sure the Alliance will be poring over every detail of the report. I fear that if the Astral Express gets dragged into matters, leaving will not be an easy task.

**符玄**：Mm. Jing Yuan has committed quite a few violations this time around, and I'll have to deal with them one by one... *sigh* The Cloud Knight generals are all such a hassle.

**符玄**：Before you leave, please make time to recuperate. If there's anywhere you'd like to visit, feel free to take a look. I must attend to some Cloud Knight matters — farewell for now.

**符玄**：Oh — if you pass through Starskiff Haven, I have something that I hope you can give to Yukong.

---

#### 分支 3

**符玄**：*sigh* It was all so chaotic. When Phantylia took form, it was as if Tingyun vanished into thin air.

**符玄**：The Cloud Knights were only able to find her fan — she took it with her everywhere. It's currently unclear if Tingyun was a puppet manipulated by Phantylia, or if some form of deception was used to cloud our vision.

**符玄**：I'm already prepared for the worst. Considering how the Legion operates, I fear the fate of the Sky-Faring Commission Amicassador may be a bleak one.

**符玄**：As for how matters are handled with regards to Tingyun, I believe it's best left to her foxian kin. I have informed the Sky-Faring Commission of the events which took place, and I think it would be best if you were the one to deliver this item to Yukong.

**瓦尔特**：...I understand. Leave it with us.

**符玄**：Thank you, Mr. Yang. Now, let me take a look at my remaining duties for the day. *sigh* It's quite the list...

**符玄**：It's getting late. If you need me, I'll be in the Divination Commission Conclave Hall.

## Quest Summary

安灵布奠，天清路远是第二章仙舟罗浮的最后一个子任务，是第二章的收尾任务。讲述了大战结束后列车组前往神策府探问情况、符玄暂代将军职责并感谢列车组、列车组成为罗浮的誓助盟友、与符玄梳理建木灾厄始末（幻胧是主使，将星核交给药王秘传引发叛乱）、符玄托付列车组将停云的扇子送交驭空、停云的真实情况（可能被幻胧操纵或杀害，凶多吉少）、整理停云留下的物品发现尚未送出的礼物、代停云送礼物给不夜侯的梦茗和尚滋味的燕翠、青雀介绍金人巷的过程。

### Key Info

- **符玄暂代将军**：景元受伤，符玄暂代将军职责，处理战后事务
- **誓助盟友**：列车组成为罗浮的誓助盟友，受到视同联盟使节的最高规格优待
- **幻胧的阴谋**：幻胧是一切的主使，伪装潜入，将星核交给药王秘传，引发叛乱，想诱使罗浮自灭
- **停云的命运**：和列车组同行的停云可能是受幻胧操纵的傀儡或幻术，本人凶多吉少
- **素鲤扇**：停云留下的扇子，符玄托付列车组送交驭空
- **停云的礼物**：停云留下了尚未送出的礼物，包括给梦茗的茶种和给燕翠的礼物
- **遏云歌**：梦茗按停云的提议新制的茶饮，取了停云的名字
- **金人巷**：长乐天的商业街，有很多好吃的好玩的地方
- **建木的处置**：除了帝弓的光矢，没什么能伤其根本，依靠持明族的力量或许能加以抑制

### Character Relations

- **符玄与景元**：符玄暂代景元的将军职责，要替他处置违规之举
- **符玄与列车组**：符玄感谢列车组，授予他们誓助盟友的身份
- **停云与梦茗**：停云答应给梦茗找新茶种，梦茗新制了「遏云歌」取名纪念停云
- **驭空与停云**：驭空在悲伤中撑持，力主举办仪式安抚伤痛

### Foreshadowing

- **停云的真实命运**：她是被幻胧操纵了，还是已经被杀害了？她还活着吗？
- **景元的伤势**：他能恢复吗？谁来为他医治？
- **建木的未来**：它会被如何处置？持明族能抑制它吗？
- **药王秘传的残党**：还有多少残党尚未被清剿？
- **星核的位置**：星核深埋在建木中的哪里？
- **幻胧的威胁**：反物质军团还会再次进攻吗？
- **持明族的纠葛**：持明族内有什么纠葛牵扯？
- **丹恒的身份**：他与持明族有什么关系？
- **列车组的下一站**：他们接下来要去哪里？
