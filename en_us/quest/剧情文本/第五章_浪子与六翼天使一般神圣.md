## 一、拜访模糊二维马

Returning to the Trailblazer's POV...

When you have a chance to make a choice, make one that you know you won't regret.

After some time, you and March pay a visit to the author of The Fluffy...

**开拓者**：Blurry_QR_Colt... That's an unusual name.

**三月七**：Why would the author of <i>Fluffy Across the Blue</i> have such a pen name?


> **分支选项（开拓者可选择）：**
>
> 1. **画漫画画到视线模糊了。**
> 2. **指不定是什么新反派......**

---

#### 分支 1

**三月七**：You're taking it a bit too literally. But... it's not impossible!

**三月七**：Someone who can draw something that cute must have a warm, gentle heart.

**三月七**：I heard The Fluffy's been running for half a year in a magazine. At first, it wasn't very popular in Planarcadia.

**三月七**：But after that Memory Bubble movie <i>Holy Grail/stay night</i> Old Oti produced became a massive hit, the Astral Express's adventure stories spread far and wide, and The Fluffy started gaining popularity.

**三月七**：They say the latest serialized chapter, 'The Sacred Flame Returns: The Creation of Amphoreus,' has sold out everywhere.

**三月七**：This Colt sure knows a lot about our journey. Must be a galactic fan of the Express Crew.

#### 分支 2

**三月七**：Come on, what ever happened to trust?

**三月七**：According to the address the Pearluxe employee gave us, it looks like we've arrived at a familiar place...

**开拓者**：For such a small place, Dovebrook District is really full of hidden talents.

**三月七**：Turn left at the first intersection, then turn right. Go straight for two hundred meters. Wait, this is...

## 二、虚照就是模糊二维马

Step into the newspaper office and see how Blurry_QR_Colt truly looks like

Lift your veil, show me your face. That expression, smug and mocking, just like the full moon high in the sky.

**三月七**：The Fluffy goes puff-puff-puff, Pom-Pom's fur? Extra fluffy stuff.

**狸猫**：Evil boss!

**狸猫**：Pay us!

**虚照**：Ahem. Please come in.

**虚照**：Let me guess... Your next line is—

**虚照**："Why are you here!?"


> **分支选项（开拓者可选择）：**
>
> 1. **是你！**
> 2. **得找她要版权费才行。**
> 3. **别装了。**

---

#### 分支 1

**虚照**：What's the problem? I'm the magazine's founder, its president, and its editor-in-chief. It's only logical that I'd be sitting here.

**普狸策**：Wha? A million?! President Xuzhao has been owing this raccoon... no, owing all of us brothers our wages for months now! If it weren't for the money... who would risk their neck working as a tabloid journalist, snapping paparazzi shots of the Astral Express's big shots?

It finally occurs to you why the name "Nihilux" always felt vaguely familiar. The former president of <i>Furbobo Weekly</i>, that deadbeat boss who skipped town to dodge her debts, went by that exact name.

**普狸策**：A boss who doesn't pay wages has no right to sit here, they belong in the underworld!

**虚照**：Idiots. Without me, you'd have long since been locked away in some painting-prison by the 'Department of Aberration Defense.' And you still have the nerve to demand wages from the very benefactor who protects you.

**虚照**：Trailblazer, have you heard of the sensational social news — the 'Phantom Thief Shapeshifter Raccoon' serial theft case? Now, back in Planarcadia year 1...6—

**普狸策**：I'm sorry! I was wrong! I shouldn't have talked back! I'm sorry for being born a furbo!

**幻太子**：Bip-bop! Even if you've got dirt on us, making us work for free is going way too far, even for raccoons!

**普狸策**：We've successfully staged a mutiny from below! We have a new president! President Trailblazer, please uphold justice for us!

**虚照**：Welcome, Nameless! Furbo hotpot requires a reservation, rumors are <unbreak>10,000</unbreak> credits a piece, and if you're looking to hire a hitman, just head right down the hall to the storeroom. Oh, or maybe—

**虚照**：You came to meet the brilliant, charming, and universally adored Blurry_QR_Colt? Well then... truth be told, that person is actually—

---

#### 分支 2

**虚照**：Haha, who could it be?

---

#### 分支 3

**开拓者**：Once you rule out all the wrong answers, only one truth remains—

**开拓者**：The only one here who can actually draw is you, Nihilux. You're Blurry_QR_Colt!

**虚照**：...

**虚照**：Ah, you saw right through me. As a reward for seeing through my disguise, how about an autograph or a photo?

**虚照**：Allow me to reintroduce myself. I'm Nihilux, you can also call me by my pen name, Blurry_QR_Colt.

**三月七**：No wonder you were tagging along back at Pearluxe Tower, acting like you knew us so well. Wait...

**三月七**：This "Blurry_QR_Colt" persona isn't a fake too, is it?

**虚照**：Identity is just a mask and a body. Real or fake, it's all in how you see it.

**虚照**：At the very least, for now, there's no other Blurry_QR_Colt around who could show up at Star Rail FEST for a live signing and hype up the Astral Express, right?

**三月七**：So you already know why we're here?

**虚照**：Just a little deduction, that's all.

**虚照**：Regarding 'Star Rail FEST' — overwhelming publicity has begun flooding the internet. Given Lady Pearl's attitude of pursuing perfection in everything, she will surely create more favorable conditions for your cooperation.

**虚照**：Naturally, having me step in was part of her plan.

**虚照**：That said, I'll have to respectfully decline.

**虚照**：Not to brag, but <i>Fluffy Across the Blue</i> is ridiculously popular in this world. If tickets weren't limited, the line would wrap around the planet twice and a half.

**虚照**：That's not a signing event, but a public execution, right?

**三月七**：Yeah, that does sound pretty horrifying.

**所以，什么时候更新？**

**虚照**：Could be tomorrow, could be next month, but definitely not today.

## 任务总结

浪子与六翼天使一般神圣是第五章「二相乐园」的第十个任务，是第二部分"献给破晓的失控"的核心任务。讲述了开拓者和三月七拜访《苍天航路绒绒号》的作者模糊二维马、来到《狸狸周刊》报社、虚照是《狸狸周刊》的创始人兼社长兼总编（拖欠全体兄弟好几个月工资、如果没有他狸猫们早就被异常防御部关到画中监狱去了）、虚照就是模糊二维马（排除所有错误选项后真相只有一个）、星铁FES展会（真珠邀请模糊二维马做现场签售为星穹列车造势）、虚照拒绝了（《苍天航路绒绒号》的人气如果不限票现场的人排起来能绕整个星球两圈半、这哪儿是签售会这不就是上刑场吗）、虚照说所谓身份不过是一张面具和一副皮囊是真是假只取决于看待它的角度的过程。

### 关键信息

- **模糊二维马**：《苍天航路绒绒号》的作者，真实身份是虚照
- **虚照**：《狸狸周刊》的创始人兼社长兼总编，逃债失踪的甩手掌柜
- **《苍天航路绒绒号》**：在二相乐园大火的漫画，最新连载《圣火归还之章·翁法罗斯创世》卖到脱销
- **《圣杯/今晚留下来》**：老奥帝先生投拍的忆泡电影，大卖后星穹列车的冒险故事广为人知
- **《狸狸周刊》**：虚照创办的报社，位于鸽川区
- **普狸策**：狸猫记者，被拖欠工资，想下克上
- **幻太子**：狸猫，说让他们打白工太狸了个大谱
- **怪盗变化狸**：轰动一时的社会新闻连环失窃案
- **异常防御部**：会把狸猫关到画中监狱
- **星铁FES**：珠星财团举办的展会，邀请模糊二维马签售
- **真珠**：万事都谋求尽善尽美，邀请虚照出马
- **鸽川区**：《狸狸周刊》报社所在地，卧虎藏龙
- **老奥帝**：投拍忆泡电影的人
- **珠星大厦**：虚照之前跟着星穹列车的地方
- **下克上**：狸猫们想让开拓者当新社长
- **狸肉火锅**：虚照说请提前预约
- **小道消息**：一万信用点一条
- **买凶杀人**：往里直走右拐杂物间里住的家伙就是干这个的

### 角色关系

- **虚照与模糊二维马**：同一人，虚照是真实身份，模糊二维马是笔名
- **虚照与狸狸周刊**：创始人兼社长兼总编，拖欠工资
- **虚照与真珠**：真珠邀请他参加星铁FES，他拒绝了
- **虚照与星穹列车**：在珠星大厦时老跟着他们，对他们很熟悉
- **普狸策与虚照**：被拖欠工资，想下克上
- **三月七与虚照**：怀疑模糊二维马这个身份也是冒名顶替的
- **开拓者与虚照**：识破了他的伪装
