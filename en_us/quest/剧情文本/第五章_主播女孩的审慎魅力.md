## 一、星期日的调律失控

**爻光**：Your thoughts tell me that you still have doubts about me.

**爻光**：Did you ask to meet here so I could clear up some of your doubts?

**星期日**：...Greetings, Lady Yao Guang.

**爻光**：Being able to hear others' thoughts anytime... this "tuning" ability sure comes in handy.

**星期日**：Compared to the Seer Strategist's ability to come and go as she pleases, this is nothing but a parlor trick.

**星期日**：I've heard that Xianzhou folks are big on etiquette. Yet here I am, and you're still playing hide-and-seek. General, isn't that a bit discourteous?

**爻光**：Sorry... I have this old problem of suddenly disappearing. It's really something beyond my control.

**爻光**：As for the reason, I'm afraid I can't disclose it. Please forgive my rudeness.

**爻光**：Or, if you're truly that curious, why not use your "tuning" and find out?

**星期日**：You know I won't do that.

**星期日**：Besides, I couldn't even if I wanted to right now.

**爻光**：Couldn't?

**星期日**：At this moment, the crowd's thoughts are crashing over me like a tidal wave.

**星期日**：I can't block out all this mental noise. Just keeping my own thoughts from getting tangled up in everyone else's is hard enough.

**爻光**：I see. So that's why, when you were speaking with Miss Himeko earlier, I accidentally intruded on your "private conversation."

**星期日**：My "tuning" is spiraling out of control. This mask... is distorting my abilities.

**爻光**：...I see it differently. The loss of control is temporary. That mask is amplifying your power.

**星期日**：How is this possible? This "tuning" power is bestowed by the Harmony. How could the Elation meddle with THEIR authority?

**爻光**：Perhaps the Elation simply poured more "fuel" into this divine authority?

**爻光**：In my humble opinion, this mask links the Supplicants with all beings of Planarcadia, drawing upon the power of the same Path here for your use.

**爻光**：That's why you're hearing others' thoughts surge toward you like a tide... Your "tuning" is steadily expanding, and you've become the center of this melody.

**星期日**：You sure gave me quite the "gift," general.

**星期日**：But why would such a precious gift be placed on my face...? I need an honest answer.

## 二、爻光为什么不戴面具

**爻光**：You want to know the reason? Didn't I explain it already?

**星期日**：Your reasons about me make sense, but what I really want is your answer... about yourself.

**星期日**：General of the Yuque who sees into the future... why not wear the mask yourself and personally step into the fray with its power?

**爻光**：Words can deceive, but only "tuning" lays the truth bare.

**爻光**：Mr. Sunday, why not use your enhanced "tuning" to feel the weight of that answer for yourself?

**星期日**：Well then... pardon my intrusion, Lady Yao Guang.

**爻光**：Ah... so this bird on my shoulder is the manifestation of the "tuning's" resonance?

**星期日**：Exactly. And these symbols I see floating above people's heads...

**星期日**：...Would be the "Weal and Woe Signs" you mentioned, general?

**爻光**：That's right. To become an Arbiter-General, one must undergo three ceremonies: the "Rainbow Ordainment," the "Reignbow's Gaze," and the "Bestowal of Spiritus." Only then can one truly take up the mantle and live up to the title.

**爻光**：When I was appointed as the Yuque's general, I was bestowed with the Reignbow Spiritus called "Kālacakra Skyplume," which represents the vision of the Hunt among the seven aspects of the Reignbow: a symbol of foresight, verdict, and self-discipline.

**爻光**：Thanks to that, I gained the "Eye of Īśvara," which allows me to read the flow of destiny.

**爻光**：Since the day I arrived on this world, every person I've seen bore a crimson "Woe Sign" above their head without exception. Within days at most, a catastrophic disaster will befall this place.

**星期日**：So, that red glow rising from the edge of the city is...?

**爻光**：The blazing light formed by the convergence of all those "Woe Signs" in Planarcadia.

**星期日**：In that case, general, it's all the more reason for you to wear the mask. Once your sight into the future is strengthened, you won't need to fear the "deathly omen" in your readings.

## 三、事不二卜的铁律

**爻光**：Mr. Sunday, have you heard the diviners' iron law? "Never divine the same matter twice"?

**星期日**：Even Penacony's fortune-tellers warn their customers before a card reading that they can't demand a do-over just because the result doesn't suit them. Is there some particular significance to this?

**爻光**：Divining the future is, in essence, an act of observation and calculation — an interference with the future. And each divination pushes the chaotic, unclear future toward a definite collapsed outcome, even if that outcome is not a welcome one.

**爻光**：To be honest, when I set out for Planarcadia, I had fully intended to personally participate in the Phantasmoon Games.

**爻光**：But the moment I laid hands on the mask, I understood the consequences of wearing it. Once this miraculous item is donned, the "Eye of Īśvara" would transcend mere sight and permeate throughout this realm. The Kālacakra Skyplume would predict the future of all beings at tens of billions of instances per second.

**星期日**：...You're saying that, simply by your observation, our future would become fixed?

**爻光**：Exactly — just as Nous, the All-Wise Lord, expounds the ways of heaven and anchors the fated moment.

**爻光**：If I wore the mask, I would become the Nous of Planarcadia, and I would "personally anchor" a destiny that drives us all into a bottomless darkness. We will become locked in a dead end with not a shred of hope for salvation.

**爻光**：Close one. I passed the trial. There's still a sliver of hope in this round.

## 四、与神博弈

**星期日**：...Now that makes me even more curious.

**星期日**：Pardon my bluntness, but the fate of billions in Planarcadia never had much to do with you, General. Your encounter with the Express and the IPC was merely a chance meeting.

**星期日**：You are a messenger of the Xianzhou. Since you foresaw the omen — that "an Arbiter General will fall here" — you could have boarded your ship and left at any time...

**星期日**：Why choose to stay and help us? To help this world break free of this deathtrap?

**爻光**：Because... I am guided by great compassion. Every life is precious beyond measure...

**爻光**：Such diplomatic pleasantries won't fool the former master of Penacony like yourself, would they?

**爻光**：Then, how about this reason? The Astral Express has distinguished itself time and again against the Destruction, and bears an irreplaceable duty for the future. I can't just sit idly and watch you face annihilation...

**星期日**：...

**爻光**：*sigh* Give me a break. I really am terrible at all this diplomatic talk.

**爻光**：Mr. Sunday, what makes you think that everything I've done is just to save Planarcadia?

**星期日**：What?

**爻光**："Weal is where Woe lurks. Woe is where Weal leans." The good fortune of some often births the ill fate of others, and a dead end for someone may hide an opportunity for another.

**爻光**：This treacherous land where life and death turn again and again, where fortune flips to misfortune, is, in my eyes, the perfect board for a game of wits.

**星期日**：A game with whom?

**爻光**：...With the gods.

## 五、三十年前的方壶血战

**爻光**：Mr. Sunday, have you heard of the "Battle of the Fanghu" thirty years ago?

**星期日**：I've heard rumors of that war, but not the details. General, are you suggesting Planarcadia is now facing dangers just as perilous as the Xianzhou Fanghu was back then?

**爻光**：You got that right.

**爻光**：Thirty years ago, the Abundance Axis broke our lines and invaded the Fanghu homeland. The abominations awakened a living demonic star, intending to sink the Xianzhou Fanghu.

**爻光**：The Alliance, at its wits' end, took a desperate gamble — adopting the plan my junior sister proposed: operating the star-gazing instrument, the 'Cloud-Gazing Mirror,' to send a signal to the Divine Archer for aid, awaiting his light-arrow to shoot down the calamitous star.

**爻光**：Whoever operated the Cloudpeer Telescope would not return. It was to be a one-way trip.

**星期日**：So your junior soror...?

**爻光**：You guessed wrong. The one who offered his life was my master, who taught me divination. It was Master Jingtian, then the Yuque's Master Diviner, and the only high official authorized to operate that device. He accepted his fate and ventured to the Fanghu.

**爻光**：Going with him into certain death were Yueyu, the previous Yaoqing General who guarded the Cloudpeer Telescope, and the Verdant Knights under her command.

**爻光**：While I was charged with assessing the plan's feasibility...

## 任务总结

主播女孩的审慎魅力是第五章「二相乐园」的第七个任务，是第一部分"欢迎来到二相乐园"的核心任务（星期日视角）。讲述了爻光和星期日碰头、星期日的调律能力正在失控（四周人群的思想像海潮一样涌向他、没法隔绝杂讯）、爻光说面具在增强星期日的力量（欢愉为同谐的权能注入更多燃料、面具将谒者和二相乐园的众生联为一体抽汲此间相同命途的力量供你们使用、星期日成了这曲旋律的中心）、星期日问爻光为什么不把面具扣在自己脸上、爻光让星期日用增强过的调律亲自体会答案的重量、爻光的帝弓威灵时轮天雉明王（于帝弓七相中代表巡猎的眼界、是预视决断与自制的象征、靠着它才有了判读气运的观自在眼）、二相乐园所有人头顶赤红的凶签（最迟不过数日将有一场灭顶灾变到来、城市远方升起的红光是所有人凶签汇聚而成的光焰）、事不二卜的铁律（占卜未来本质上是观测与计算是对未来的干涉、每次测算都会将混沌不清的未来推向某个确定的坍缩结果）、爻光如果戴上面具（观自在眼将超脱目光所限遍入此界、时轮天雉明王会以每秒百亿次计判读众生的未来、就像遍智天君博识尊阐演天道锚下命定的时刻、会亲眼将诸位推入黑暗的命运囚锁在命定的死局里再无一点拯救的希望）、爻光说与神博弈（福兮祸之所伏祸兮福之所倚、这个生死百转否泰错变的凶危之地在我眼中恰恰也是绝佳的博弈之地）、三十年前的方壶血战（丰饶孽物联军杀入方壶本土、唤醒活化的妖星意欲击沉方壶仙舟、采纳爻光师妹提出的计划操作瞰云镜向帝弓发射信号求援、负责操作瞰云镜的人将有去无回十死无生、自愿成仁者是爻光的师父时任玉阙太卜的竟天先生、与他一同踏入死地的还有前代曜青将军月御以及她所率领的青丘军士卒、爻光负责勘定整个计划的可行性）的过程。

### 关键信息

- **调律失控**：星期日的调律能力正在失控，四周人群的思想像海潮一样涌向他
- **面具增强力量**：欢愉为同谐的权能注入更多燃料，面具将谒者和二相乐园的众生联为一体
- **旋律的中心**：星期日的调律不断扩张，他已成了这曲旋律的中心
- **时轮天雉明王**：爻光的帝弓威灵，于帝弓七相中代表巡猎的眼界，是预视决断与自制的象征
- **观自在眼**：判读气运的能力，靠着时轮天雉明王才拥有
- **帝弓天将三道仪式**：天虹封典、帝弓垂眸、受赐威灵

- **事不二卜**：卜者的铁律，每次测算都会将混沌未来推向确定的坍缩结果
- **每秒百亿次**：爻光戴上面具后时轮天雉明王判读众生未来的频率
- **博识尊**：遍智天君，阐演天道锚下命定的时刻
- **亲眼推入死局**：爻光戴上面具会亲眼将诸位推入黑暗的命运囚锁在命定的死局里
- **与神博弈**：爻光留下来的真正原因，这个凶危之地是绝佳的博弈之地
- **方壶血战**：三十年前的战争，丰饶孽物联军杀入方壶本土
- **活化的妖星**：孽物们唤醒的，意欲击沉方壶仙舟
- **瞰云镜**：观星器具，向帝弓发射信号求援
- **竟天先生**：爻光的师父，时任玉阙太卜，唯一有权限操作瞰云镜的重臣，自愿成仁
