## 1. Before the Gates of Okhema

**Trailblazer**: (That is... the Titankin of Nikador? What are they doing here?)

**Sky-Felling Hunter**: Pa... Paris...

**Iason**: I—I don't know any Paris! Don't hurt me or Heracles!

**Dromas "Heracles"**: Roar! Roaaaar!

**Captain Camilla**: Stand back! Damned remnants of the mad king Nikador, you shall advance no further!

**Sky-Felling Hunter**: Kill...


> **Branch Options (Trailblazer's choice):**
>
> 1. **You're not killing anybody!**
> 2. **A treasure? Why didn't you say so earlier!**
> 3. **I get it. Let me handle it.**

---

#### Branch 1

Faced with an unstoppable power, the injured Titankin turns and flees, slipping into the Evernight Veil outside the city.

Its staggering and broken form is so pathetic that you lose all urge to chase it down for a victorious counterattack. So, you simply watch as her shadow disappears into the border where the light vanishes altogether.

**Captain Camilla**: The fact that the Lance of Fury underlings would be so cowardly as to flee in defeat. Pathetic... Thank you for saving us, hero. We would not have made it out of here alive without you.

**Captain Camilla**: Ever since the fall of the savage god, this Titankin has been roaming the surrounding area, attacking guards and passing trade caravans. This time, it was Iason, leader of the Argo Caravan, who fell victim.

**Iason**: That's right. Fortunately, you and Captain Camilla took action in time! If I were sent to meet Thanatos, no one would be left to support the city's merchants or dromases!

**Dromas "Heracles"**: Groan...

**Iason**: It's okay, Heracles... Ah, though I've been spared my life, she did get away with my little treasure...

---

#### Branch 2

**Iason**: That thing is no ordinary item — it's the sacred relic of Mnestia, the golden thread she wove with her own hands!

**Iason**: You've heard this story, haven't you? In the Age of Creation, Mnestia spun the world's love and her own remnant cocoon into silk threads, as a gift for Cersia.

**Iason**: Lady Aglaea sent me on an assignment to retrieve the miracles of the Titans. I worked myself to the bone to purchase this object from a group of nomadic thieves. Now, it's been snatched from my hands by the enemy right before I arrived back at the city... Such a shame...

Iason drops his head with a sigh, his face painted with regret. It seems he is stuck between the sadness of having lost the precious item and the guilt of disappointing Aglaea... However, you sense his gaze darting between the ground's surface and your body.

---

#### Branch 3

**Iason**: That's wonderful. Thank you, hero! Thank you, hero!

**Iason**: Um... while traveling for trade, I heard from refugees that the Titankin all gather in Castrum Kremnos beneath the black tide — only heroes like you know where it is.

**Captain Camilla**: Hero, as the black tide outside the city is perilous, we mere citizens can offer you no assistance. All we can do is ask you to take great caution.

**Mem**: A treasure hunt! Partner, let's go on a treasure hunt!

## 2. In Search of the Sky-Felling Hunter

**Mem**: I can smell a sweet fragrance... No, this is the fragrance of an unknown memory! It's coming from over there!

After their ferocious battle with you, the Titankin from before seem to have lost their previous murderous intent.

It's at this moment that you realize just how ancient its stone-cast body is. It's just as old and derelict as the walls of Castrum Kremnos under the curtain of night.

**Sky-Felling Hunter**: Pa... ris...


> **Branch Options (Trailblazer's choice):**
>
> 1. **Hand over the golden thread!**
> 2. **It's not on the map.**
> 3. **Resurgence of the Past!**

---

#### Branch 1

**Sky-Felling Hunter**: The golden thread... Paris'... It was stolen...

**Mem**: Huh? Are you saying this belongs to you?

**Sky-Felling Hunter**: It's... Paris...

**Mem**: Looks like she won't hand over the treasure unless I find this "Paris" person... Where could he be?

**Sky-Felling Hunter**: He went... To Arcadia...

**Mem**: Alright! Off we go! To Arcadia to find... Wait — where is Arcadia?

---

#### Branch 2

**Sky-Felling Hunter**: Pa... Paris...

The Titankin's voice is growing ever weaker. It seems she no longer has the mental capacity to answer your questions, only to silently repeat that stranger's name to herself.

**Mem**: Well now we're stuck! We don't know this person, or this place!

---

#### Branch 3

**Mem**: Go back to her past to find clues? That's a great idea!

**Mem**: But, this statue lady's memory is too blurry. We need to find other fragments of it if we want to see into her past. Partner, let's go to Marmoreal Market and collect some memories related to "Paris" and "Arcadia"!

## 3. Asking Around at Marmoreal Market

Go to Marmoreal Market and ask about "Paris" and "Arcadia"

**Mem**: The store owners here meet all kinds of people every day. They'll be the most knowledgeable about the area. We should ask some of them.

### Ask Demetria at the fruit shop

**Demetria**: Hello, young friend! Are you here to buy fruits? These are all in fresh from the "garden," and they're fresh as can be!


> **Branch Options (Trailblazer's choice):**
>
> 1. **I want to ask you some questions.**
> 2. **Do you know Paris?**
> 3. **Have you heard of Arcadia?**

---

#### Branch 1

**Demetria**: Questions? Haha, no one has come to me seeking enlightenment since I left the Garden of Wisdom. Please, ask away.

---

#### Branch 2

**Demetria**: Paris? I have never heard that name before.

**Demetria**: I am certain that he doesn't live in Okhema. People from all over this holy city have frequented my store and I remember the name of every customer!

---

#### Branch 3

**Demetria**: Arcadia? Is that a fruit? Sorry, young one, I don't sell Arcadia here.

**Demetria**: But if you can find some seeds, then I can ask my friend in the Garden of Wisdom to plant some. Then you can come and buy some next year!

**Mem**: Looks like she didn't have anything useful to offer. Let's go and ask another store owner...

### Ask Ctesiphona at the bookstore

**Ctesiphona**: Praise Cerces! May you assist the people of this holy city in understanding the charm of my works...

**Ctesiphona**: Ah, a customer! I was just praying to Cerces to gift intelligence to my fellow citizens. Is there a book you are after?


> **Branch Options (Trailblazer's choice):**
>
> 1. **I want to ask you some questions.**
> 2. **Do you know Paris?**
> 3. **Have you heard of Arcadia?**
> 4. **How do we get to Arcadia?**

---

#### Branch 1

**Ctesiphona**: Of course! Imparting knowledge is the very mission entrusted to my family by Cerces! I will share any knowledge I have!

---

#### Branch 2

**Ctesiphona**: Paris... Paris... I've never heard that name? Wait!

Ctesiphona rummages for some time among slates and scrolls. It seems that the most inconvenient thing about working with slates and scrolls is that there is no front cover to help identify them quickly...

**Ctesiphona**: Phew... I checked poems, mythology, plays, and adventures, but no mention of this person. Looks like I won't be able to help you.

---

#### Branch 3

**Ctesiphona**: Arcadia? Customer, you're a well-read one. That's a setting beloved by the adventure tales of ages past — the golden land sheltered by Mnestia!

**Ctesiphona**: It's a paradise for poets, artists, and those in search of romance. The main characters in these stories would meet their true love here and receive Mnestia's blessing... This is all recorded in the classic work <i>Secret of the Golden Carpet.</i>

**Ctesiphona**: Speaking of the <i>Secret of the Golden Carpet</i>, that story of a prince eloping with the princess of an enemy state is truly a masterpiece...

Ctesiphona begins to endlessly talk about the story to you. If you don't get back to the matter at hand, she may end up recounting the entire epic.

---

#### Branch 4

**Ctesiphona**: As for its location, every story says something different. Some say that it is at the center of Amphoreus. Some say it is in the ocean depths of Phagousa... Some even say that it is on the back of a flock of butterflies, floating high in the skies.

**Ctesiphona**: As for which is true, I have no idea... They're all stories, after all.

**Mem**: I didn't realize Arcadia was such an amazing place! Although, descriptions in stories probably still aren't enough, and we still know nothing about Paris... Let's go and find someone else to ask.

### Ask Aphrea and Aglaea at the tailor's shop

**Aglaea**: ...I know that those who revere Mnestia are fond of the so-called "golden thread dress," but weaving clothing out of malleable gold not only makes it very expensive, it also makes it very hard for the wearers to move in. Would it not be better to use dyed silk or cotton?

**Aphrea**: But everyone says Mnestia's silk...

## Quest Summary

"I Once Was in Arcadia" is an Adventure Quest in Chapter 4 "Amphoreus." It tells the story of the Trailblazer helping Captain Camilla repel the Sky-Felling Hunter at the gates of Okhema, accepting Iason's commission to find the stolen Golden Thread of Rebirth, tracking down the Sky-Felling Hunter to learn about "Paris" and "Arcadia," asking around at Marmoreal Market (Demetria at the fruit shop, Ctesiphona at the bookstore, and Aphrea and Aglaea at the tailor's shop), entering the prison of Castrum Kremnos and meeting Paris, watching Paris flee with the Sky-Felling Hunter, and learning about the past through memory fragments.

### Key Info

- **Okhema**: The holy city of Amphoreus, home to Marmoreal Market
- **Castrum Kremnos**: Where Nikador's followers gather, beneath the black tide
- **Nikador**: The Mad King, one of the Titans, who has fallen
- **Sky-Felling Hunter**: A follower of Nikador with a stone-cast body in the form of a woman, searching for "Paris"
- **Paris**: A smooth-talking man imprisoned in Castrum Kremnos, entangled with the Sky-Felling Hunter in the past
- **Arcadia**: The golden land sheltered by Mnestia, a paradise for poets, artists, and those who chase romance; its location is unknown
- **Golden Thread of Rebirth**: Mnestia's sacred relic, created by her own hands — she spun the world's love and her own remnant cocoon into silk threads as a gift for Cersia
- **Iason**: The boss of the Argo Caravan, commissioned by Aglaea to collect the miracles of the Titans
- **Aglaea**: A Chrysos Heir who funded Iason's commission to collect the miracles of the Titans
- **Mnestia**: One of the Titans, the goddess of the Age of Creation
- **Cersia**: One of the Titans, the goddess of wisdom
- **Thanatos**: God of the underworld river, symbol of death
- **Heracles**: Iason's dromas
- **Camilla**: The guard captain of Okhema
- **Demetria**: Owner of the fruit shop at Marmoreal Market, from the Garden of Wisdom
- **Ctesiphona**: Owner of the bookstore at Marmoreal Market, a transmitter of knowledge
- **Secret of the Golden Carpet**: A classic adventure tale about a prince eloping with the princess of an enemy state
- **Resurgence of the Past**: Mem's ability to glimpse the past through memory fragments

### Character Relations

- **Sky-Felling Hunter and Paris**: The two share a tangled past, and the Sky-Felling Hunter has been searching for Paris
- **Iason and Aglaea**: Aglaea funded Iason's commission to collect the miracles of the Titans
- **Demetria and the Garden of Wisdom**: Demetria hails from the Garden of Wisdom and keeps ties with old friends there
- **Ctesiphona and Cersia**: Ctesiphona's family was entrusted by Cersia with the mission of passing on knowledge

### Foreshadowing

- **Paris's true identity**: Who is he? What past does he share with the Sky-Felling Hunter?
- **The true location of Arcadia**: Where is it? Does it really exist?
- **The secret of the Golden Thread of Rebirth**: Why is it so important? How is it connected to Paris and the Sky-Felling Hunter?
- **The Sky-Felling Hunter's past**: What did she go through? Why has she been searching for Paris all this time?
- **The fall of Nikador**: How did he fall? Why do his followers still roam?
- **The bond between Mnestia and Cersia**: The Golden Thread of Rebirth was a gift for Cersia — what story lies between them?
- **Aglaea's goal**: Why does she want to collect the miracles of the Titans?
- **The first memory**: What happened at the gates of Okhema in days past? What is the story of Elder Oenone and Guard Captain Philoctetes?
