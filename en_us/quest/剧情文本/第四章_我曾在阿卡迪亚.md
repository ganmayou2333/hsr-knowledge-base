**开拓者**：(That is... the Titankin of Nikador? What are they doing here?)

**天谴猎手**：Pa... Paris...

**伊阿宋**：I—I don't know any Paris! Don't hurt me or Heracles!

**大地兽「赫拉克勒斯」**：Roar! Roaaaar!

**戍卫克米昂**：Stand back! Damned remnants of the mad king Nikador, you shall advance no further!

**天谴猎手**：Kill...


> **分支选项（开拓者可选择）：**
>
> 1. **你谁也杀不了！**
> 2. **有宝物？不早说！**
> 3. **我懂，让我来吧。**

#### 分支 1

Faced with an unstoppable power, the injured Titankin turns and flees, slipping into the Evernight Veil outside the city.

Its staggering and broken form is so pathetic that you lose all urge to chase it down for a victorious counterattack. So, you simply watch as her shadow disappears into the border where the light vanishes altogether.

**戍卫克米昂**：The fact that the Lance of Fury underlings would be so cowardly as to flee in defeat. Pathetic... Thank you for saving us, hero. We would not have made it out of here alive without you.

**戍卫克米昂**：Ever since the fall of the savage god, this Titankin has been roaming the surrounding area, attacking guards and passing trade caravans. This time, it was Iason, leader of the Argo Caravan, who fell victim.

**伊阿宋**：That's right. Fortunately, you and Captain Camilla took action in time! If I were sent to meet Thanatos, no one would be left to support the city's merchants or dromases!

**大地兽「赫拉克勒斯」**：Groan...

**伊阿宋**：It's okay, Heracles... Ah, though I've been spared my life, she did get away with my little treasure...

---

#### 分支 2

**伊阿宋**：That thing is no ordinary item — it's the sacred relic of Mnestia, the golden thread she wove with her own hands!

**伊阿宋**：You've heard this story, haven't you? In the Age of Creation, Mnestia spun the world's love and her own remnant cocoon into silk threads, as a gift for Cersia.

**伊阿宋**：Lady Aglaea sent me on an assignment to retrieve the miracles of the Titans. I worked myself to the bone to purchase this object from a group of nomadic thieves. Now, it's been snatched from my hands by the enemy right before I arrived back at the city... Such a shame...

Iason drops his head with a sigh, his face painted with regret. It seems he is stuck between the sadness of having lost the precious item and the guilt of disappointing Aglaea... However, you sense his gaze darting between the ground's surface and your body.

---

#### 分支 3

**伊阿宋**：That's wonderful. Thank you, hero! Thank you, hero!

**伊阿宋**：Um... while traveling for trade, I heard from refugees that the Titankin all gather in Castrum Kremnos beneath the black tide — only heroes like you know where it is.

**戍卫克米昂**：Hero, as the black tide outside the city is perilous, we mere citizens can offer you no assistance. All we can do is ask you to take great caution.

**迷迷**：A treasure hunt! Partner, let's go on a treasure hunt!

## 二、寻找天谴猎手

**迷迷**：I can smell a sweet fragrance... No, this is the fragrance of an unknown memory! It's coming from over there!

After their ferocious battle with you, the Titankin from before seem to have lost their previous murderous intent.

It's at this moment that you realize just how ancient its stone-cast body is. It's just as old and derelict as the walls of Castrum Kremnos under the curtain of night.

**天谴猎手**：Pa... ris...


> **分支选项（开拓者可选择）：**
>
> 1. **把金丝交出来！**
> 2. **地图上没写。**
> 3. **往世复现！**

---

#### 分支 1

**天谴猎手**：The golden thread... Paris'... It was stolen...

**迷迷**：Huh? Are you saying this belongs to you?

**天谴猎手**：It's... Paris...

**迷迷**：Looks like she won't hand over the treasure unless I find this "Paris" person... Where could he be?

**天谴猎手**：He went... To Arcadia...

**迷迷**：Alright! Off we go! To Arcadia to find... Wait — where is Arcadia?

---

#### 分支 2

**天谴猎手**：Pa... Paris...

The Titankin's voice is growing ever weaker. It seems she no longer has the mental capacity to answer your questions, only to silently repeat that stranger's name to herself.

**迷迷**：Well now we're stuck! We don't know this person, or this place!

---

#### 分支 3

**迷迷**：Go back to her past to find clues? That's a great idea!

**迷迷**：But, this statue lady's memory is too blurry. We need to find other fragments of it if we want to see into her past. Partner, let's go to Marmoreal Market and collect some memories related to "Paris" and "Arcadia"!

**克帖西芬**：Of course! Imparting knowledge is the very mission entrusted to my family by Cerces! I will share any knowledge I have!

---

#### 分支 2

**克帖西芬**：Paris... Paris... I've never heard that name? Wait!

Ctesiphona rummages for some time among slates and scrolls. It seems that the most inconvenient thing about working with slates and scrolls is that there is no front cover to help identify them quickly...

**克帖西芬**：Phew... I checked poems, mythology, plays, and adventures, but no mention of this person. Looks like I won't be able to help you.

---

#### 分支 3

**克帖西芬**：Arcadia? Customer, you're a well-read one. That's a setting beloved by the adventure tales of ages past — the golden land sheltered by Mnestia!

**克帖西芬**：It's a paradise for poets, artists, and those in search of romance. The main characters in these stories would meet their true love here and receive Mnestia's blessing... This is all recorded in the classic work <i>Secret of the Golden Carpet.</i>

**克帖西芬**：Speaking of the <i>Secret of the Golden Carpet</i>, that story of a prince eloping with the princess of an enemy state is truly a masterpiece...

Ctesiphona begins to endlessly talk about the story to you. If you don't get back to the matter at hand, she may end up recounting the entire epic.

---

#### 分支 4

**克帖西芬**：As for its location, every story says something different. Some say that it is at the center of Amphoreus. Some say it is in the ocean depths of Phagousa... Some even say that it is on the back of a flock of butterflies, floating high in the skies.

**克帖西芬**：As for which is true, I have no idea... They're all stories, after all.

## Quest Summary

我曾在阿卡迪亚是第四章「翁法罗斯」的一个冒险任务，讲述了开拓者在奥赫玛城门前帮助戍卫克米昂击退天谴猎手、接受伊阿宋的委托寻找被抢走的蜕生金丝、找到天谴猎手了解"帕里斯"和"阿卡迪亚"的线索、去云石市集打听（水果店德墨忒尔、书店克帖西芬、裁缝铺阿芙罗与阿格莱雅）、进入悬锋城牢房遇到帕里斯、帕里斯带着天谴猎手逃跑、通过记忆碎片了解过去的故事。

### Key Info

- **奥赫玛**：翁法罗斯的圣城，有云石市集
- **悬锋城**：尼卡多利眷属聚集的地方，在黑潮之下
- **尼卡多利**：疯王，泰坦之一，已陨落
- **天谴猎手**：尼卡多利的眷属，石铸身躯，女子模样，在寻找"帕里斯"
- **帕里斯**：花言巧语的男人，被关在悬锋城牢房，与天谴猎手有过去的纠葛
- **阿卡迪亚**：墨涅塔庇护的黄金之乡，诗人、艺术家与追逐浪漫之人的天堂，位置不明
- **蜕生金丝**：墨涅塔的圣物，她亲手创造的，将世上的爱与自己的残茧捻作丝线，作为赠予瑟希斯的礼物
- **伊阿宋**：阿尔戈商队的老板，受阿格莱雅委托收集泰坦神迹
- **阿格莱雅**：黄金裔，出资委托伊阿宋收集泰坦神迹
- **墨涅塔**：泰坦之一，造物世的神
- **瑟希斯**：泰坦之一，智慧之神
- **塞纳托斯**：冥河之神，死亡的象征
- **赫拉克勒斯**：伊阿宋的大地兽
- **克米昂**：奥赫玛的戍卫长
- **德墨忒尔**：云石市集水果店老板，来自智慧花园
- **克帖西芬**：云石市集书店老板，知识传递者
- **《金毯密传》**：经典冒险故事，王子与敌邦公主的私奔故事
- **往世复现**：迷迷的能力，可以通过记忆碎片窥见过去

### Character Relations

- **天谴猎手与帕里斯**：两人有过去的纠葛，天谴猎手一直在寻找帕里斯
- **伊阿宋与阿格莱雅**：阿格莱雅出资委托伊阿宋收集泰坦神迹
- **德墨忒尔与智慧花园**：德墨忒尔来自智慧花园，与那里的老朋友有联系
- **克帖西芬与瑟希斯**：克帖西芬家族受瑟希斯使命传递知识

### Foreshadowing

- **帕里斯的真实身份**：他是谁？他与天谴猎手有什么过去的纠葛？
- **阿卡迪亚的真实位置**：它在哪里？它真的存在吗？
- **蜕生金丝的秘密**：它为什么这么重要？它与帕里斯和天谴猎手有什么关系？
- **天谴猎手的过去**：她经历了什么？她为什么一直在寻找帕里斯？
- **尼卡多利的陨落**：他是怎么陨落的？他的眷属为什么还在徘徊？
- **墨涅塔与瑟希斯的关系**：蜕生金丝是赠予瑟希斯的礼物，她们之间有什么故事？
- **阿格莱雅的目的**：她为什么要收集泰坦神迹？
- **第一场的记忆**：旧日的奥赫玛城门发生了什么？俄诺涅元老和菲罗克戍卫长的故事是什么？
