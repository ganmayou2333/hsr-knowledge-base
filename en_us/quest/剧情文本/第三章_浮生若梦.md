## 一、翡翠与托帕的讨论

Switching to Jade's POV...

**托帕**：...Old Oti is a tricky opponent. I didn't expect him to take the risky step of "going public" at such a critical moment for Penacony.

**翡翠**：Indeed, he's definitely bold. It's that kind of boldness that made him the Oti Alfalfa he is today.

**翡翠**：Still, the outcome is uncertain. Shouting loud doesn't necessarily carry any weight. What about the phone call I asked you to make, Topaz?

**托帕**：Ah, they agreed, but it'll take some time before they arrive.

**翡翠**：Just as it should be. The "sweet dew" should be served after the "bitter poison."

**托帕**：Looks like we'll be skipping the "exchanging apples" step this time around.

**翡翠**：Now that we're dealing with a greedy merchant, a simple apple wouldn't make a difference. Well, I guess I included myself in that remark too.

**翡翠**：Now I'm a bit curious, Topaz. Do you think Penacony is a quality asset?

**托帕**：Yes, despite its recent calamity, Penacony remains a top-quality asset within the Cosmos, with good credit, lucrative potential, and promising prospects.

**翡翠**：Well, that's obvious, but what I truly wanted to know is: This project is obviously too <b>bland</b> for your taste, isn't it?

**托帕**：That's true. I wouldn't be here if it weren't for Aventurine.

**翡翠**：But despite that, you trust him. You even entrusted him with a Cornerstone, something as precious as life itself, to finish this gamble.

**托帕**：Are you not in the same boat, Miss Jade? Without us playing along, your "Jade Stone" wouldn't have made it across the border so easily, allowing you to see all desires that flow through dreams to gain a bargaining chip in negotiations...

**托帕**：That's why I'm willing to stake my "Topaz Stone" to cover for you... It's like one big elaborate game of chess.

**翡翠**：Once that kid sets his mind to something, nothing can stop him, not even fate. Well, at least he's still alive, and that's the best outcome.

**托帕**：Looks like... we've strayed off-topic, Miss Jade. Should we discuss our next steps?

**翡翠**：No need. I'll go it alone. Meanwhile, you can go greet our honored guest and wait for my message.

**托帕**：Yes sir.

## 二、与知更鸟打声招呼

**翡翠**：(Is that... Robin? She's also here, on the Radiant Feldspar.)

**翡翠**：Greetings, Miss Robin. I didn't expect to meet you here.

**知更鸟**：Miss Jade? Greetings. The opening ceremony for the Charmony Festival has been moved to the Radiant Feldspar, so I'm here making some preparations.

**知更鸟**：How about you? Have you spoken with Mr. Alfalfa?

**翡翠**：I'm actually on my way to meet him right now. Do you know him well, Miss Robin?

**知更鸟**：Unfortunately, I never met him — I only heard a few brief comments from... Sunday, the former head of the Oak Family: Mr. Oti is a formidable businessman, but in other respects... less than complimentary.

**翡翠**：Where do you think the future of the Planet of Festivities is headed?

**知更鸟**：I believe the sweet dream will see its rebirth, just like the Radiant Feldspar resumed its voyage.

**知更鸟**：The Harmony needs a new direction. Only by bidding farewell to the past can we actually sail into the future.

**知更鸟**：There are no permanent allies or everlasting enemies. So, let's both... take what we need from this deal.

**翡翠**：Naturally. I'm looking forward to your performance. See you at the Festival.

**知更鸟**：See you later, Miss Jade.

## 三、与老奥帝的会谈

Keep talking with Oti Alfalfa

**翡翠**：Thank you for your patience, Mr. Alfalfa. Let's continue our discussion.

**老奥帝**：Ohohoho, figured out something already, Miss Jade? Hmm... but where is Miss Topaz?

**翡翠**：Topaz has something else to take care of, you'll be seeing her later. Talks can still continue between the two of us.

**老奥帝**：Is it just me, Miss? Your tone sounds... very different now.

**翡翠**：I need to set a good example for my junior. It's not a good habit to be too loose-lipped during negotiations, right?

**翡翠**：Now, we can speak frankly and openly. Do you believe what I said, Oti? You're not the only merchant who has seen the changes in the cosmic market over the past ten Amber Eras.

**老奥帝**：Interesting... Ohohoho, now that's interesting.

**老奥帝**：Good! It's good to be straightforward! Openness and transparency are my things! So, tell me, what's your next move?

**翡翠**：Unfortunately, I'd like to speak the harsh truth before laying out my plan. Let's cut to the chase. First, your plan won't work. Penacony has no way of sidestepping the IPC and going public.

**翡翠**：Second, you can't stop the IPC from entering Penacony. We've got all the time and connections in the world to find a way in. We'll keep tearing down and rebuilding this place until the Asdana system gets used to the IPC's ways again.

**翡翠**：Now I'm repeating your words exactly. If you don't want to be a laughingstock and have everyone gunning for you at the official conference... you'd better drop your little pie-in-the-sky plan.

**老奥帝**：Ohohohohoho! Interesting! Indeed!

**老奥帝**：You surely have a way with words! Now I'm curious to know what you have up your sleeve.

**翡翠**：Mr. Alfalfa, let's not forget that the IPC controls the biggest interstellar publicity platform. More than half the news networks in the universe take their orders from us.

**翡翠**：The moment news spreads about Penacony going public, trillions of customers will immediately receive a message like this:

**翡翠**：'The Family's protection over Penacony has failed — accidents in dreams may now result in permanent brain death.'

**翡翠**：Care to guess how many ways we have to turn Alfalfa credits into worthless junk within a measly <unbreak>24</unbreak> system hours? With the entire cosmos keeping a close eye on Penacony, I assure you it won't be too hard.

**老奥帝**：You really think you can pull that off?

**翡翠**：Even from Pier Point, as distant as it may be, I'm more than capable of keeping you on a tight leash.

**翡翠**：However, if you agree to give up that half-baked plan to go public, the IPC will assure you that we'll never jeopardize the interests of the family heads under your leadership.

**翡翠**：In Asdana, we need allies too. The Corporation can 'help' Penacony with financing — starting with purchasing 30% of its equity. With our capital flowing in, Penacony's stability and reconstruction would be no more than an overnight project.

**老奥帝**：30%? Who can guarantee your appetite won't keep growing?

**翡翠**：Heh, that's the brilliant part of it all. The answer is simple: No one. There are no guarantees. It all hinges on self-awareness and mutual respect. However, the board of directors will consider the interests of the family heads to some extent.

**翡翠**：You're a smart merchant, Old Oti. Isn't the whole purpose of this elaborate game to showcase your business acumen and seek more benefits for The Family?

**翡翠**：It benefits us if we both take a step back. And if that's not enough for you, I'm pretty sure that another goal of making Penacony go public is to expand the influence of the Planet of Festivities and attract more customers.

**翡翠**：I understand your concern, and I have a solution for that, too.

**老奥帝**：Fine, now I see your sincerity. As the head of the Alfalfa Family, I don't think I have any reason to refuse your offer...

**老奥帝**：But as one of His chosen, I still need one final answer.

**翡翠**：Go ahead, I'm listening.

## 任务总结

浮生若梦是第三章「匹诺康尼」的任务，切换至翡翠视角，讲述了翡翠和托帕讨论老奥帝的"上市"险棋、翡翠与知更鸟打招呼（知更鸟说同谐需要找到新的方向，没有永远的盟友也没有永远的敌人）、翡翠与老奥帝·艾弗法的会谈（翡翠威胁老奥帝放弃上市计划，公司掌握着星际间体量最大的宣传平台，可以在24个系统时里把苜蓿币贬得一文不值）、公司承诺买进30%股权帮助匹诺康尼融资、老奥帝问公司身为琥珀王信徒为何不站在秩序一方、翡翠解释信用点和货币体系（每有一个世界加入信用体系版图，寰宇和平事业便再添一砖一瓦）、托帕问翡翠"鸩毒"的消息来源、翡翠说来自一位心系同谐未来的女孩（作为交换要帮她完成一件事）、翡翠说最后一位"贵客"也该登上这艘船了的过程。

### 关键信息

- **老奥帝·艾弗法**：苜蓿草家系家主，在匹诺康尼最危急关头走出"上市"险棋
- **翡翠**：公司战略投资部，慈玉典押主人，翡翠石基石能力，谈判核心角色
- **托帕**：石心十人，托帕石基石能力，为翡翠作掩护
- **砂金**：拉托帕入局，完成一场赌局
- **上市计划**：老奥帝想让匹诺康尼绕过公司公开募股，翡翠威胁要让苜蓿币贬得一文不值
- **公司的宣传平台**：掌握星际间体量最大的宣传平台，全宇宙新闻网络超过一半是公司喉舌
- **30%股权**：公司买进匹诺康尼30%股权，帮助融资和重建
- **信用点**：星际间唯一被认可的恒定价值货币，公司有能力保障它永不贬值
- **货币体系**：公司的目标是让所有外汇、资本、贸易都归于统一的货币体系之下
- **寰宇和平**：每有一个世界加入信用体系版图，寰宇和平事业便再添一砖一瓦
- **鸩毒**：翡翠得到的消息，来自一位心系同谐未来的女孩
- **心系同谐未来的女孩**：给翡翠提供消息的人，作为交换翡翠要帮她完成一件事
- **甘露与鸩毒**：翡翠说甘露的上桌顺序本就该在鸩毒之后
- **交换苹果**：托帕说这次交换苹果的环节要被省略了
- **知更鸟**：在晖长石号上准备谐乐大典，说同谐需要找到新的方向
- **晖长石号**：星穹列车，谐乐大典开幕典礼搬至此处
- **阿斯德纳**：匹诺康尼所在的星系
- **苜蓿币**：匹诺康尼的货币
- **庇尔波因特**：公司所在地，翡翠说鞭子很长即便在庇尔波因特也能勒住老奥帝脖子
- **琥珀王**：存护星神，公司的信仰
- **希佩**：同谐星神
- **太一**：秩序星神，已陨落
- **古琥珀纪**：古老的岁月，同谐希佩飞升秩序太一陨落的时代
