## 一、爻光拜访星穹列车

（爻光拜访星穹列车，与姬子等人对话）

**爻光**：这么玄乎。自在之人虽然拥有远胜凡人的选择权，但依旧身处命数洪流之中。我只想提醒你，慎重选择。

**姬子**：Back to business. I take it you're not just here to read our fortunes?

**爻光**：I'm here to greet everyone, to apologize to the Express Navigator for my previous "indiscretions," and to borrow someone from you.

**星期日**：(Miss Himeko, now you understand how I ended up wearing this mask?)

**姬子**：(I get it. She says she wants to borrow you for an operation. I want to hear your thoughts. If you're not comfortable refusing, I can speak up for you...)

**爻光**：You two, no need for whispers. I can hear you loud and clear!

**星期日**：How did you... Well, since the general heard us, would you mind telling us your reasons for doing this?

## 二、爻光在收集变数

**爻光**：I'm collecting "variables." During this journey, I'll be acting as unpredictably and irrationally as possible.

**爻光**：Don't be surprised. Some Arbiter Generals excel in battle with their wit and valor, others master the art of forging weapons, and some guard the netherworld with their lives. I, however, fight for the Alliance by calculating and manipulating fate itself, peeking into the future.

**爻光**：I'm here to discuss matters regarding the Anti-Destruction Alliance, and then I'll head to Astropolis for the vote. That's the real business.

**爻光**：But according to the divination, this arcadian world faces an extraordinary danger. When a bird's nest falls, no egg will remain unbroken.

**姬子**：...Are you saying we'll all perish along with Planarcadia? What's going on here?

---

## 三、刃的威胁

**爻光**：情报指出，仙舟重犯试图加入幻月游戏——此人身受丰饶令使「倏忽」血肉的侵染，危险至极。

**爻光**：依照本座解读：他压抑已久的凶性终将难以遏制，会为所有人带来灭顶之灾。

**丹恒**：...There's no way he could do that.

**爻光**：That's the divination's answer. Rather than asking "whether he could," we should ask, "How would he do it?"

**爻光**：My divination shows "Dragons clash in the wilderness, their ichor dark and yellow." While the term "dragon clash" could simply mean "fierce battle," could it be foreshadowing a great calamity from your conflict with Blade?

**爻光**：Perhaps this prophecy has other interpretations... But with my limited intel right now, I have to prioritize Blade, this most volatile and dangerous factor.

**姬子**：I've heard that different interpretations of prophecies can lead to completely opposite outcomes. Until things become clearer, it's better to reserve judgment.

---

## 四、刃亲手所锻的造物

**爻光**：Let's put that aside for now. In any case, I've brought along a "creation forged by Blade himself." If things get out of hand, it might temporarily restore his sanity, giving us a brief chance at victory.

**丹恒**：...What is it?

**爻光**：I'm sorry, but fate has shifted once again... I must take my leave.

**爻光**：为了让列车诸位放心，我可以指天信誓，星期日先生不必为仙舟尽任何义务，相反，我将为他提供使团力所能及的一切——

**姬子**：What did you say, general?

**丹恒**：..."Aid within our power?" She... She's gone.

**停云**：Whoa, that was quick... Madam Yao must be really busy.

**姬子**：Has she vanished into thin air like this before?

**停云**：Yes... I don't know the details myself. With Madam Yao it often happens, and no one knows when she'll return. She doesn't do it on purpose. Allow me to apologize on her behalf.

## 五、姬子的安排

**姬子**：No worries, at least we know what the Xianzhou is up to.

**姬子**：眼下，列车有两位同伴仍被困在某处，我和开拓者必须动身去「酒馆」查清一些线索。

**停云**：If Miss Himeko needs my help, please don't hesitate to ask.

**丹恒**：Are March and Mr. Yang in trouble? I'll go with you.

**姬子**：No, you and Sunday will act together with the Seer Strategist. We're not at the point where we need to call in reinforcements yet.

**姬子**：The situation with Blade is unusual. That Seer Strategist's concerns aren't just baseless worries.

**姬子**：I don't want to see the Phantasmoon Games bring catastrophe to my homeland, so this is also my request. To put it in the general's words: Please do your best to introduce new "variables"!
