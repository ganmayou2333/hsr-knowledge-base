## 一、抵达星槎海中枢

你们搭乘星槎来到了星槎海中枢…

**停云**：On behalf of the Sky-Faring Commission, allow me to welcome you to Starskiff Haven.

**停云**：On the Xianzhou Luofu, anything relating to aviation, navigation, and trade falls within the scope of the Sky-Faring Commission, which is one of the six commissions of the Luofu. As the biggest port on the ship, Starskiff Haven is governed by the Sky-Faring Commission, too.

**三月七**：You definitely sound like a... what was it again...? Oh, Amicassador! By which I mean — nice speech.

**停云**：That's too kind of you. My job involves ferrying business delegates to and from Starskiff Haven, so I do this kind of introduction all the time.

**停云**：Anyway, we're safe now. Normally I'd be a good host and take you around. But these are difficult times. Let's head to the Palace of Astrum first and report your arrival to Madam Yukong.

**三月七**：Palace of Astrum?

**停云**：Right there, the tallest building in the city. It's also the headquarters of the Sky-Faring Commission. Let's hurry. We haven't got much time.


> **分支选项（开拓者可选择）：**
>
> 1. **你为啥这么着急？**
> 2. **那个停云好像非常狡猾…**
> 3. **好耶，逛街咯！**
> 4. **杨叔是担心这次面见仙舟的长官，我们会重蹈贝洛伯格的覆辙嘛…？**
> 5. **你对我的误会可大了。**

---

#### 分支 1

**停云**：*sigh* I don't mean to rush you, but if Madam Yukong holds me accountable for not getting you over soon enough, I wouldn't want to be around for the consequences...

**三月七**：Sounds scary. I thought you said she was kind-hearted?

**瓦尔特**：We need to make a few preparations before paying a visit to any of the six commissions. Rest assured, Miss Tingyun, we won't go anywhere. If you could inform Madam Yukong of our arrival first, we'll meet you at the palace entrance later.

**停云**：If you insist...

停云点头致意后离开了…

**瓦尔特**：Little miss fox moves at quite the pace — I can't keep up with her. *sigh* I've spent too long on the bench... Let's catch our breath for a while — we can expect a packed schedule ahead.

---

#### 分支 2

**瓦尔特**：That might be a little harsh, but she does put her own interests first.

**瓦尔特**：She did the right thing by not trusting us initially — we were an unknown quantity to her. As for the rush... my guess is she's just eager to get us out of her hair and further up the chain.

---

#### 分支 3

**瓦尔特**：Not so fast...

**瓦尔特**：I need to give you two a heads up first. Our meeting with Madam Yukong won't be without risk. We need to be prepared and take it seriously.

---

#### 分支 4

**你是说，驭空就是幕后黑手！？**

**瓦尔特**：No, the Xianzhou Alliance is better than that. But Madam Yukong will definitely have a string of difficult questions for us...

**瓦尔特**：Why did we choose to come to the Xianzhou now? How did we know that the incident is related to a Stellaron? Who leaked their information to us...?

**瓦尔特**：Careless answers mean distrust and potential hostility, which would make things much harder for us.

**三月七**：这可糟了，我和开拓者都是笨嘴笨舌，一不小心就说错话的类型……

---

#### 分支 5

**三月七**：*sigh* See?

**瓦尔特**：Don't worry. We'll find a way through. Let me do the talking.

## 四、谒见驭空

**航务官**：Yes, Madam.

**驭空**：Report the losses to General Jing Yuan and find someone from the Divination Commission. They can't just stand there watching while we deal with this mess...

**驭空**：Greetings, guests from the Astral Express.

**驭空**：Tingyun has informed me of the purpose of your visit. Receiving guests isn't normally one of my responsibilities...

**驭空**：...but since you know about the Stellaron and have stated that you want to help the Luofu, I thought I should at least meet you in person...

**驭空**：...and politely decline your proposal.


> **分支选项（开拓者可选择）：**
>
> 1. **拒绝！？**
> 2. **……**

---

#### 分支 1

**驭空**：区区星核而已，联盟早已知悉此物，自有办法应对。仙舟翾翔八千载，见惯了危急存亡。眼下的灾难虽来势汹汹，仙舟亦有余力自处，不需假借外人之力来平息祸端

**驭空**：You are guests from afar — there is no reason for this matter to concern you... Am I making myself clear?

**瓦尔特**：From what we've gathered, the influence of the Stellaron can still be contained.

**瓦尔特**：If we can locate it in time and contain it, it's possible that we can restore any space — and any individuals — affected by the corrosion.

**瓦尔特**：We have experience in preventing Stellaron disasters, and we've only come here to lend you that experience.

**驭空**：I've said this and I'll say it again: This is an internal affair of the Xianzhou Alliance and there is no need for the Astral Express to get involved. I decided to arrange a meeting with you as a gesture of respect, but my decision is final.

**瓦尔特**：...But—

**三月七**：Never mind, Mr. Yang. If the Xianzhou Alliance say they can handle it, then we're wasting our breath. Let's go.

**驭空**：I'm afraid that's impossible.

**三月七**：...Huh? And what's that supposed to mean?

**驭空**：It's only been a few days since the presence of a Stellaron was detected on the Luofu. Starskiff Haven has been under full lockdown — no one has been permitted to leave. How did you come to know of the incident, and what led you to conclude that it was linked to a Stellaron?

**驭空**：I acquired the access history of Starskiff Haven. Not long ago, someone hacked into the system and opened the Jade Gate, before guiding you, the Astral Express, into our territory.

**驭空**：The hacker was skillful and even taunted us with her seal. Her name is Silver Wolf, a member of the Stellaron Hunters...

**驭空**：What say you in your defense?

## 任务总结

紫府通谒，将军定策是第二章仙舟罗浮的第三个子任务，讲述了列车组抵达星槎海中枢、停云介绍天舶司、前往司辰宫谒见驭空、驭空拒绝帮助并质疑列车组与星核猎手的关系、景元将军出现替列车组解围、景元同意列车组在仙舟自由行动并委托捉拿卡芙卡、景元与符玄彦卿的对话、停云带列车组去浥尘客栈的过程。

### 关键信息

- **天舶司**：仙舟六司之一，执掌空域、航行和贸易事务，星槎海在其治下
- **司辰宫**：天舶司的总部，城里最高的建筑
- **驭空**：天舶司司舵，执掌航行与外交，性格严肃果断
- **景元**：罗浮将军，神策将军，云上五骁之一，睿智沉稳，喜欢下棋
- **符玄**：太卜司之首，太卜，性格直率，想继任将军
- **彦卿**：云骑骁卫，景元的侍卫，天才剑士，想成为剑首
- **银狼**：星核猎手的一员，骇入系统打开玉界门，故意留下印戳挑衅
- **六司**：仙舟的六个部门，各掌其职
- **求药使**：来自星海各处想找到仙舟人延续生命秘密的人
- **浥尘客栈**：天舶司为列车组订的客栈

### 角色关系

- **驭空与景元**：驭空是天舶司司舵，景元是将军，驭空对景元尊重但也有自己的坚持
- **景元与符玄**：符玄想继任将军，景元说她心智上还要再磨磨
- **景元与彦卿**：彦卿是景元最信任的人，景元想让他耐下性子，不可随意动手
- **停云与驭空**：停云畏惧驭空，想尽快把列车组这个麻烦丢给上级
- **景元与列车组**：景元信任列车组，委托他们捉拿卡芙卡

### 伏笔

- **银狼的印戳**：她为什么要故意留下印戳挑衅？星核猎手在策划什么？
- **景元的棋局**：他说"这棋局中的暗手还没揭开"，他的计划是什么？
- **星核的位置**：它如何掩人耳目，绕过天舶司的核查与太卜司的推演？
- **卡芙卡的下落**：景元委托列车组捉拿卡芙卡，她在哪里？
- **刃的身份**：景元审问刃，刃说"人有五名，代价有三个"，这是什么意思？
- **驭空的过去**：她为什么对星核如此警惕？她是否有过相关的经历？
