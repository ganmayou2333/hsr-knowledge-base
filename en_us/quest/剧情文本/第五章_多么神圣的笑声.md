## 一、真珠讨论幸研会

（真珠与开拓者等人讨论幸研会的问题）

**你想问**：The Smiles Society has grown into what it is today — were you all truly unaware of it?

**真珠**：I had put them on a temporary watch list before, due to significant decreases in crime rates in certain regions.

**真珠**：But now, we finally know how they achieved such a miracle.

**真珠**：Personally speaking, I cannot fault the citizens who joined the Society, nor do I wish to fixate on the accountability of those who governed at the time.

**真珠**：After all, for the corporation, the Smiles Society is a tool for maintaining stability, and the members are simply following the human instinct to avoid suffering.

**真珠**：Trailblazer, you've been to Penacony, so you should know: there, Family members use the power of 'Harmony' to lull people into dreams and soothe their pain. From the standpoint of human ethics, a moderate escape from pain is an unimpeachable right.

**真珠**：Humans cannot shut off their innate 'negative feedback' the way artificial life-forms can — I deeply understand and sympathize with this.

## 二、幸福手术

**真珠**：My assigned theme is "Analytical Art." But at the same time, I am fortunate to be appointed governor of a world.

**真珠**：The portrayal and sublimation of pain in human works of art shine among humanity's greatest treasures. Tragedy, elegy, and lost epics... Their soul-stirring power springs from a profound understanding of this "negative feedback."

**真珠**：This so-called "Happiness Surgery" does more than just obliterate people's perception of pain. It strips away human complexity, and turns what was dynamic and brilliant into a monotonous blotch.

**真珠**：To me, governing this world is like creating a painting. My vision is to maintain the diversity of lifeforms and preserve their potential for growth and change. The Research Society has disrespected my work.

**真珠**：Thus, my duty and theme have become one and the same: I will do all that I can to cleanse the misguided strokes of the Research Society from this land.

**真珠**：Thank you again for lending your aid, Nameless. I will begin extrapolation of our next steps now.

**我还想和在场的各位聊聊。**

**真珠**：Please, be my guest.

## 三、不死途发现真珠在利用他

**不死途**：You know, I'm not here to be an ornament.

**真珠**：Assessment: You have something to ask me, Mr. Ashveil.

**不死途**：Lately, I've been on an extremely lucky streak. Be it Fulwish or Magnus, the leads practically gained sentience and came to me one by one.

**真珠**：Luck is also an important part of one's toolkit. Wouldn't you agree?

**不死途**：I'm beginning to think you're the one behind it all. You tasked me to investigate the Society and protect the Astral Express, which all led to me unleashing my shadow on those people... just so you can confirm your hypothesis.

**不死途**：...You had everything planned out since the beginning, hadn't you?

**真珠**：If I answer "Yes," would it wound your ego?

**不死途**：...Perhaps.

**真珠**：Yes. I paved the way for your investigation, leading you straight to the suspects.

**真珠**：Through you, I confirmed my suspicions about the Research Society. However, the question of Abundance Transformation remains a thorny issue for me.

**真珠**：Fortunately, there's a ranger who specializes in confronting such abominations, having lived in long seclusion within Planarcadia.

**不死途**：Oink...

## 四、不死途手臂中的影子

**不死途**：But you know the "shadow" sealed inside my arm... can be very dangerous.

**不死途**：Simply removing the nail can cause it to stir and resonate with the "torn scroll" and fissure.

**不死途**：Had I not kept it under control at that time, the fight would have ended with all of us buried in that bottomless abyss. Is that part of your plans too, Pearl?

**真珠**：Humans fear the worst, but to the Intellitrons, such fears are merely irrational deterrents.

**真珠**：Wasn't the Mask of Elation I gave you enough to pin down the "shadow"?

**真珠**：—That Express Navigator... the 'Stargazer' Wojian guided you to find the 'gift.'

**真珠**：—That shadow which, ten Amber Eras ago, swallowed swarms and the Lord Ravager 'Zhuluo.'

**不死途**：You won the bet. You know...

## 任务总结

多么神圣的笑声！是第五章「二相乐园」的第十一个任务，是第二部分"献给破晓的失控"的结局任务。讲述了真珠讨论幸研会（曾因部分地区犯罪率显著降低而将幸研会列入短期观察、无法苛责加入幸研会的公民也不想追究当时管理者的过失、对群体而言幸研会是维持稳定的称手工具对会众来说逃避痛苦是人类的天性、匹诺康尼家族成员会以同谐的力量催人入梦安抚人们的痛苦适度的逃避痛苦是无可厚非的权利、人类无法像智械一样关闭与生俱来的负反馈）、幸福手术（抹去的不仅仅是人类痛苦的感知、它篡改了人类的复杂性将人类这幅斑斓的画作简化成了单一的色块、管理世界即是作画维持其上生命形态的丰富与演化潜力、幸研会亵渎了我的作品、职责与母题合为一题会尽我所能清除幸研会在这片土地上留下的错笔）、不死途发现真珠在利用他（总觉得自己的行动格外幸运线索就像自己长了腿一个个地往跟前靠、派他去调查幸研会保护星穹列车最终将一切导向让他解放影子吞掉那几人以验证对幸研会的猜想）、真珠承认是她为不死途铺平了调查的路（透过他证实了对幸研会的猜想、但丰饶转变这个答案仍令她感到棘手、二相乐园隐居着一位隐退许久的游侠之首他对处理这类凶煞孽物很有经验）、不死途手臂中的影子（封印在手臂中的影子实在很危险、光是拔出钉子残卷和裂隙就和它产生了共鸣躁动了起来、如果当时没能制住它这场战斗会把所有人葬送在那个深不见底的空间里）、欢愉假面给予力量钉住影子（观星者我见指点寻获的馈赠、那道在十个琥珀纪前吞下过虫群与绝灭大君诛罗的影子）的过程。

- **幸研会**：幸福微笑研究会，通过幸福手术抹去人类痛苦的感知
- **幸福手术**：抹去人类痛苦的感知，篡改人类的复杂性
- **真珠的母题**：解析艺术，同时治理这个世界
- **管理世界即是作画**：真珠的理念，维持生命形态的丰富与演化潜力
- **负反馈**：人类与生俱来的，无法像智械一样关闭

- **欢愉假面**：真珠交给不死途的，给予足够力量钉住影子
- **观星者我见**：指点不死途寻获馈赠的人
- **十个琥珀纪前**：影子吞下虫群与绝灭大君诛罗的时间
- **绝灭大君诛罗**：被影子吞下的绝灭大君
- **虫群**：被影子吞下的
- **满愿**：幸研会的领袖，不死途调查的对象
- **真弘**：不死途调查的另一个对象
- **错笔**：真珠说要清除幸研会留下的错笔
