## 一、流萤和花火的接头

Check the dolls in the area

**「接头娃娃」**：Password, password — answer at once: Why do the living sleep?

**流萤**：...

**流萤**：Because people are afraid to wake up from the dream?

**「接头娃娃」**：Perfect, identity confirmed. Welcome, Agent Mech Tycoon. Please proceed to the next rendezvous point. Our people will be waiting for you there.

**流萤**：Is this... really necessary?

**「接头娃娃」**：Gotta stay in character! Follow the instructions, Mech Tycoon!

**「接腿娃娃」**：Hello, please submit identity verification for Agent Ultra Firepower Instant Star Traveler.

**流萤**：...Who's there!?

**「接腿娃娃」**："Mech Tycoon" wasn't cool enough, so...

**花火**：So Miss Sparkle went ahead and changed it!

**花火**：Masked Fool, you shouldn't be here.

**花火**：Well, someone's been kept in the dark this whole time, completely oblivious to their real buddy standing right in front of them.

**流萤**：...Buddy?

**花火**：Don't tell me you still haven't noticed? That Memokeeper has already stabbed you in the back once!

**花火**：You're kidding... Silver Wolf never mentioned me even once? But she's the one who invited me to team up.

**花火**：It was me who found out that the Family is all-knowing and all-powerful in the sweet dream. Trying to welsh on the deal?

**流萤**：Your intel...

**流萤**：(With Kafka's Spirit Whisper, my cognition shouldn't be susceptible to distortion. Could things be different now that I'm in a dream?)

**流萤**：Before heading to Penacony, Silver Wolf did tell me to keep an eye out for anyone asking "why does life slumber." But as evidence, that's not enough.

**花火**：Oh really? Then what if... you were to die three times in the sweet dream?

**流萤**：...

**流萤**：Then why is this the first time we're comparing notes?

**花火**：What else could I do? As a Fool, how could I ever catch up to a Memokeeper in a dream? Every single time, just when I was about to reach you, poof, she'd vanish with you.

## 二、康士坦丝和梦主的阴谋

**流萤**：Who exactly is Constance?

**花火**：She works for the Dreammaster, but she definitely has her own agendas. She secretly made a deal with me.

**花火**：She pointed toward a nightmare — the place where all the Dreammaster's schemes would be carried out.

**花火**：I've been looking everywhere for you just to give you this spoiler.

**流萤**：A nightmare... What exactly is in it?

**花火**：Tell you straight up? No way. I went through hell and back for this.

**花火**：At most one sentence: after you've lived through your finest moments, fly toward the sky with all your might — you'll hear the chirping of insects at the top of the oak.

**流萤**：That's way too cryptic. I can't trust you.

**花火**：Please, we're working together. I'm not some lackey. Just because someone else fooled you doesn't mean you get to pin it on me!

**加拉赫**：You're back sooner than I thought... Any results?

**姬子**：Yes... And now, it's up to us to forge ahead.

**流萤**：Sorry, I'll catch you later.

**流萤**：But... thanks. At least you brought good news.

**花火**：...Which part was good news?

**流萤**："The best of times."

## 三、寰宇蝗灾

**黑天鹅**：What does the Dreammaster gain from recreating the Swarm Disaster?

**砂金**：...Huh!?

**砂金**：Reminds me of the good old days during the War of Gods...

**砂金**：Oh, would you look at my memory — I have to go retrieve a certain 「lost item,」 the Cornerstone of Emerald. Otherwise, another lady is going to be upset.

**大丽花**：Sorry, but you're not going anywhere.

**大丽花**：This is the land chosen by Gopher Wood to "meet disaster," but he doesn't want the Swarm to truly surge forth and devour the world.

**大丽花**：...The Swarm Disaster will be trapped here, and so will you in your weakened state.

**砂金**：Unless a kind Memokeeper lends a helping hand, right?

**大丽花**：But I can't find a reason. So far, there hasn't been a single case of one of the Ten Stonehearts being killed. It's driving me crazy with curiosity!

**银枝**：Glory above! Everyone, with this 「ill omen」 before us, we must stand united! Allow me to prove my conviction with spear and blade, and break through this pall of gloom!

**银枝**：By the 「Beauty」 above — the Radiant Feldspar has carried me to this very place. Perhaps it is fate's guidance: in this dream tainted by 「Propagation,」 I shall uphold the name of Beauty and clear the path ahead for all of you!

## Quest Summary

树上的灾兆是匹诺康尼的支线任务。讲述了流萤和花火的接头（接头娃娃询问生命因何而沉睡、流萤回答因为人们害怕从梦中醒来、身份确认为特工机甲大亨、接腿娃娃要求超强火力必秒度星者的身份验证、花火说所以花火大人就改掉了呀）、花火说她是银狼主动邀请搭伙的（家族能在美梦里全知全能还是我找到的情报想赖账）、流萤的怀疑（有卡芙卡的言灵术我的认知理应不可能遭到篡改、赶往匹诺康尼前银狼的确告诉我要留意询问生命因何而沉睡的人但作为证据这还不够）、花火说你会在美梦中迎来三次死亡、康士坦丝和梦主的阴谋（给梦主办事的但一看就不老实她偷偷和我做了个交易、她指向了一个噩梦梦主的所有阴谋都会在那里实行、最多一句话在你度过最美好的时光之后就尽力飞向天空吧你会在橡木的顶端听见虫鸣）、流萤说谢谢至少你带来了好消息最美好的时光、寰宇蝗灾（黑天鹅问重现寰宇蝗灾对梦主而言这有什么好处、砂金说倒让人想起些神战时的旧事、砂金要去找回一样失物翡翠基石否则会让另一位女士不高兴、大丽花说可惜你走不掉这是歌斐木选定的降灾之地但他并不希望虫群真的奔涌而出啃食世界、寰宇蝗灾会被困在这里此刻虚弱的你也一样、大丽花说迄今为止还没有石心十人遇害的先例真让人心痒难耐）、银枝出场（荣光在上诸位恶兆当前我们更当戮力同心、我名为银枝来自纯美骑士团为消弭世间丑恶之物穿梭于银河间、希世难得号竟将我带来了此地或许正是命运的指引、在这片被繁育污浊的梦境中我将贯彻纯美之名为诸位立荡清前路、砂金说这位路过的骑士你算是找对人了我是正被困于此地的公司使节请您伸出援手吧纯美女神伊德莉拉美貌盖世无双、黑天鹅说学得倒快）的过程。

### Key Info

- **生命因何而沉睡**：接头暗号，流萤回答因为人们害怕从梦中醒来
- **特工机甲大亨**：流萤的接头身份
- **超强火力必秒度星者**：花火改掉的身份
- **花火是银狼邀请的**：银狼主动邀请花火搭伙
- **家族全知全能**：花火找到的情报，家族能在美梦里全知全能
- **三次死亡**：花火说流萤会在美梦中迎来三次死亡
- **康士坦丝**：给梦主办事的，偷偷和花火做了交易
- **梦主**：匹诺康尼梦境的主人，所有阴谋都会在噩梦里实行
- **最美好的时光**：花火的剧透，在你度过最美好的时光之后就尽力飞向天空吧
- **橡木的顶端听见虫鸣**：花火的谜语
- **寰宇蝗灾**：梦主想重现的灾难
- **翡翠基石**：砂金要去找回的失物
- **歌斐木**：选定降灾之地的存在，不希望虫群真的奔涌而出
- **石心十人遇害先例**：大丽花说迄今为止还没有
- **银枝**：纯美骑士团成员，希世难得号带来此地
- **纯美女神伊德莉拉**：纯美骑士团信仰的星神
- **希世难得号**：银枝乘坐的船
- **繁育**：污浊梦境的命途
- **加拉赫**：流萤的同伴
- **姬子**：星穹列车领航员
- **黑天鹅**：忆者，背刺过流萤
- **砂金**：石心十人，公司使节
- **大丽花**：歌斐木的使者
