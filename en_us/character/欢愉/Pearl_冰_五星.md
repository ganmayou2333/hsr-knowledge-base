## Skills

> 技能英文文本来源 Beebom Pearl Kit（4.6 beta 泄露），倍率数值与官方正式值可能存在差异，标注「beta 待核验」。Level Table 各等级精确数值待官方源回填。

### Basic ATK：Brushstroke: Trace the Severed Stream

- **Type**：Basic ATK · ST
- **Summary**：Deals Ice DMG equal to a percentage of Pearl's DEF to one designated enemy. When in possession of 【Certified Banger】, additionally deals Ice Elation DMG to the attacked enemy.
- **Effect Template**：Deals Ice DMG equal to #1[i]% of Pearl's DEF to one designated enemy. When in possession of "Certified Banger", additionally deals Ice Elation DMG equal to a percentage of Pearl's DEF to the attacked enemy targets.（倍率 beta 待核验）

### Skill：补缀生命的柔光（官方英文技能名待回填 <!-- 待译 -->）

- **Type**：Skill · Defense / Healing
- **Summary**：Gains 15 points of "Certified Banger," restores HP for all allies and additionally restores HP for the ally with the lowest HP percentage.
- **Effect Template**：Gains 15 points of "Certified Banger," restores HP for all allies equal to a percentage of Pearl's DEF plus a fixed amount, and additionally restores HP for the ally target with the lowest current HP percentage.（治疗量数值待官方最终核验：简中记 6% DEF+120，Beebom beta 记 12% DEF+240）

### Ultimate：鉴映灵魂的底色（官方英文技能名待回填 <!-- 待译 -->）

- **Type**：Ultimate · Support / Enhancement
- **Energy Cost**：180
- **Summary**：Gains 20 points of "Certified Banger." Uses "Deep Learning" on one designated ally other than this unit, making the target the "Aesthetic Archetype." Advances the target's action based on the number of Elation characters, and grants an extra turn under certain conditions.
- **Effect Template**：Gains 20 points of "Certified Banger." Uses "Deep Learning" on one designated ally other than this unit, making the target the "Aesthetic Archetype." When there are 1/2/3 or more Elation characters on the team, advances the "Aesthetic Archetype's" action by 10%/15%/30%. When there are 4 or more Elation characters on the team, the "Aesthetic Archetype" gains 1 extra turn. At the start of this extra turn, the "Aesthetic Archetype" gains 30 points of "Certified Banger" and 60 points of Punchline, which are removed at the end of the extra turn. While in "Deep Learning," Basic ATK is enhanced; if the "Aesthetic Archetype" is on the Path of Elation, additionally deals Ice Elation DMG (倍率 beta 待核验：简中记 30%，Beebom beta 记 60%). This Elation DMG is calculated using the "Aesthetic Archetype's" stats. "Deep Learning" has 3 points of Charge. After Pearl uses an Enhanced Basic ATK, 1 point of Charge is consumed. If no Charge remains after taking action, "Deep Learning" ends.

### Talent：Grow Grace from Grit

- **Type**：Talent · Defense
- **Summary**：Pearl can use "Certified Banger" as Repellency. Each point of "Certified Banger" is equal to 200 points of Repellency. When an ally target takes DMG, Pearl can consume Repellency to block 60% of the DMG for the ally target. Pearl's "Certified Banger" lasts indefinitely and has a max limit of 50. When an ally target's current HP percentage is 50% or lower, DMG taken is reduced by a percentage.（减伤比 beta 待核验：简中记 15%，Beebom beta 记 30%）

### Technique：攻击（官方英文技能名待回填 <!-- 待译 -->）

- **Type**：Technique · Attack
- **Summary**：Attacks the enemy. After entering combat, reduces the corresponding Type Toughness of enemy targets.

### Elation Skill（欢愉技）

- **Type**：Elation Skill
- **Summary**：When there are 1/2/3/4 or more Elation characters in the team, causes all ally targets to additionally deal Elation DMG of their corresponding Type equal to a percentage of the attack target after their next attack.（具体机制与数值 beta 待核验）
